Task helpers

Last full review: 2026-10-10

Prices and plans checked October 2026.

AIs that do multi-step jobs for you on their own.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

AnythingLLM

Free Easy Open source Runs on your computer

A free, open-source desktop app that turns your own documents into a private AI assistant - chat with your files and get meeting summaries, all on your device with no accounts or API keys.

Price: Free to run yourself (team cloud from $50/month)
Difficulty: Easy

The desktop app that runs on your own computer is free. Their hosted team version starts at $50 a month.
