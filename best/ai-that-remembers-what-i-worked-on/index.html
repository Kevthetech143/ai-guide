Which AI remembers what I worked on?

231 of 231 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: October 9, 2026.

Pieces quietly remembers what you work on across your computer so you can ask AI about it later, but it has no free plan and captures your screen every 2 seconds by default, while screenpipe keeps your screen, audio and meetings on your own computer so you can search what you saw or heard, with a free plan that covers recent work only. Both record your screen, so think about what is on it. Hermes Agent is different: it is an open-source assistant you install yourself and talk to from chat apps, and it remembers your projects.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Pieces

Paid Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

An app that quietly remembers what you work on across your computer so you can ask AI about it later - recaps, meeting prep, finding past work

Price: $18.99 per user/month (Pro, billed monthly; 25% off billed yearly)
Difficulty: Medium

No free plan (the 7-day trial needs a card); it captures what is on your screen every 2 seconds by default, so check its privacy settings

screenpipe

Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

Keeps your screen, audio and meetings on your own computer so you can search what you saw or heard, and share chosen context with AI tools such as Claude.

Price: Free (Basic $25/month for full history)
Difficulty: Medium

It records your screen and audio, so think about what is on screen. The free plan searches recent work only; searching your full history needs Basic at $25/month.

Hermes Agent

Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

An open-source personal AI agent you install on a Mac, Windows or Linux computer and talk to from Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, Signal or email. It remembers your projects, runs scheduled tasks and can browse the web.

Price: Free (Nous Portal Plus plan $20/month)
Difficulty: Medium

The free Nous Portal plan includes free AI models only and $0 of monthly credits. Access to 200+ models and hosted tools starts with the Plus plan at $20/month.
