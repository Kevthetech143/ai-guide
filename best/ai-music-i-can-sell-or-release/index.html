Which AI makes music I am allowed to sell or release?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

Loudly makes music from a text description with commercial licensing and can send it to Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube Music, but its free plan is for non-commercial use only. Boomy lets you make and release original AI songs with full commercial rights, though its free plan has no downloads and no commercial use. AIVA makes songs in over 250 styles, but only its Pro plan gives you full ownership; the free and Standard plans keep the copyright and limit commercial use.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.
