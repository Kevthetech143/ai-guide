Can AI help me design a flyer or poster?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

Canva AI makes flyers inside an easy drag-and-drop design tool, and Stockimg AI generates posters from typed words. Ideogram spells text inside pictures better than most other tools, which helps when a poster has words on it. Flux can make poster-style pictures too, but setting it up on your own computer takes technical steps and a strong graphics card.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Flux

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

It makes very detailed pictures from a short written description, like posters, art, or product mockups.

Price: Free; pay-per-use options
Difficulty: Medium

Setting it up on your own computer takes some technical steps and a strong graphics card.

Ideogram

Free Easy Open source

Great for making logos, posters, and designs that include words — it spells text inside pictures better than most other tools.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $15/month (billed annually; $20 monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

Pictures you make are public by default, and only the paid Plus and Pro plans can keep them private; Plus costs $20 a month, or $15 a month if you pay for a year. Free accounts get weekly credits, but the amount can change.

Official site Details

Canva AI (Magic Studio)

Free Easy Company-made

Great for making social media posts, flyers, and presentations with AI help, right inside an easy drag-and-drop design tool.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $12/month (billed annually; $18 monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan only gives up to 20 AI uses; the paid Pro plan costs $18 a month, or $144 if you pay for a year.

Stockimg AI

Paid Easy Company-made

Generate logos, posters, illustrations, wallpapers and stock-style pictures from a text description. Starter also includes AI background removal and 4x upscaling.

Price: $12/month (Starter)
Difficulty: Easy

Starter includes 300 credits a month. Unlimited credits need the Ultra plan at $48/month.
