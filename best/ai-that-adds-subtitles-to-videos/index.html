Can AI add subtitles to my video?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

Submagic, CapCut AI, Kapwing, VEED, and Wisecut are video tools that add captions or subtitles for you. Sonix and Vibe turn the speech in a video into text and export subtitles, and Whisper can too but needs command-line steps. Submagic and Sonix have no free plan, and the free plans elsewhere have limits: Kapwing's free exports carry a watermark, and Wisecut's free plan only exports at 480p.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Whisper

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

It turns spoken audio into written text, great for transcribing meetings, lectures, or video subtitles. It understands dozens of languages.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

You have to install it yourself with some command-line steps, and it needs a decent computer to run well.

CapCut AI

Free Easy Company-made

A free video editor from the maker of TikTok with AI captions, background removal, and text-to-speech built right in.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $19.99/month
Difficulty: Easy

Some features and cloud storage need the paid Pro plan, which costs $19.99 a month; CapCut is best for short videos, not full movies.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-20

Kapwing

Free Easy Company-made

Edits videos in a web browser with AI tools for subtitles, voiceovers, resizing, and background removal.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $16/month (billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

Free exports carry a Kapwing watermark and are limited to 4-minute 720p clips, and the free plan includes only 10 credits for the AI tools.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-24

Wisecut

Free Easy Company-made

Automatically cuts silences, adds subtitles and background music, and turns long videos into short clips.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from US$189/year
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan exports only 480p clips up to 3 minutes long. The Starter+ plan gives 300 credits a month, and each minute of editing uses one, so long videos use them up fast.

Official site Details

Sonix

Paid Easy Company-made

Automated transcription in 54+ languages with in-browser editing, translation, and subtitle exports.

Price: $25/month (Core)
Difficulty: Easy

Core includes 1 user and 5 hrs/month of transcription; extra seats are +$25/month each.

VEED

Free Easy Company-made

A web video editor with AI subtitles, AI voices, avatars and video generation, so you can edit and polish clips in one place.

Price: Free to start; Creator $25/month (billed monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

The Creator plan includes 500 credits a month for the AI tools. Pro costs $45/month for 2,500 credits. Paying yearly is cheaper.

Last checked 2026-10-05

Vibe

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

A free app that transcribes audio and video files into text on your own device, with subtitles export and speaker labels. Works on Mac, Windows and Linux.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

Transcription speed depends on your computer. Optional summaries use the Claude API or Ollama, which you set up separately.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-06

Submagic

Paid Easy Company-made

Upload a short video and it adds captions automatically, plus free extra clips and sound, and lets you cut the video by editing its text.

Price: $19/month
Difficulty: Easy

The $19/month Starter plan includes 45 credits (about 15 videos) a month and videos up to 2 minutes long. Automatic removal of silences and bad takes needs Pro ($39/month).
