Can AI turn my blog post or script into a video?

231 of 231 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: October 9, 2026.

Pictory turns a blog post, script, or long recording into a captioned video with stock footage matched to your words. Fliki and Lumen5 also turn a blog post into a video, and InVideo makes a finished video from a text prompt. Pictory has no ongoing free plan, only a 14-day trial.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

InVideo

Free Easy Company-made

Turn a text prompt into a finished video with stock footage and AI voiceover without editing anything yourself.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $20/month
Difficulty: Easy

Paid plans run on credits that do not roll over, and costs are hard to predict because every AI model burns credits at a different rate.

Pictory

Paid Easy Company-made

Turn a blog post, script, or long recording into a captioned video with stock footage matched to your words.

Price: $25/month (billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

There is no ongoing free plan, only a 14-day trial that exports at 720p, and paying month to month costs $29 instead of $25.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-24

Narakeet

Free Easy Company-made

Turns a written script into a spoken voiceover or narrated video in hundreds of voices and languages.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $6 (one-time, 30 minutes)
Difficulty: Easy

Free accounts cannot be used for commercial projects, and every full build (including re-doing one) eats your paid minutes.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-24

Fliki

Free Easy Company-made

Turns a script, blog post or slides into a video with a realistic AI voice, stock footage and music.

Price: Free (Standard plan $28/month, billed monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan makes 720p videos with a Fliki watermark and gives only 3 credits a month. Standard gives 180 credits a month.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-28

Lumen5

Free Easy Company-made

Turns a blog post, link or script into a short social video with AI help. Built mainly for business use.

Price: Free (Basic plan $29/month, billed monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan allows 5 videos a month, up to 2 minutes each, with a Lumen5 branded outro. The Basic plan removes the branding.
