Can AI make a voiceover for my video?

Last full review: 2026-10-10

Murf, WellSaid Labs, and Listnr turn a typed script into a voiceover for videos, and Narakeet and Fliki can build a narrated video from your script. Check the free plans first: Murf's free plan can't download your audio, and Narakeet's free accounts can't be used for commercial projects. ElevenLabs and Kapwing also make AI voices, and Kapwing does it inside a video editor.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

ElevenLabs

Free Easy Company-made

It turns your written words into natural-sounding spoken audio, great for videos, podcasts, or audiobooks.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $5/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan is small, so you will hit the monthly limit quickly if you make lots of audio.

Murf AI

Free Easy Company-made

Murf is best at turning typed scripts into natural-sounding voiceovers for videos, podcasts, and presentations.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $19/month
Difficulty: Easy

On the free plan you cannot download your audio or use it in anything commercial — downloads and commercial rights need a paid plan.

WellSaid Labs

Free Easy Company-made

Turning scripts into studio-quality voiceovers for videos and presentations.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $10/month
Difficulty: Easy

You pay for downloaded minutes of finished audio, so long projects get expensive quickly.

Narakeet

Free Easy Company-made

Turns a written script into a spoken voiceover or narrated video in hundreds of voices and languages.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $6 (one-time, 30 minutes)
Difficulty: Easy

Free accounts cannot be used for commercial projects, and every full build (including re-doing one) eats your paid minutes.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-19

Kapwing

Free Easy Company-made

Edits videos in a web browser with AI tools for subtitles, voiceovers, resizing, and background removal.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $16/month (billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

Free exports carry a Kapwing watermark and are limited to short 720p clips, and the AI features run on a limited monthly credit allowance.

Listnr

Free Easy Company-made

Turning text into natural voiceovers for videos and podcasts.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $19/month
Difficulty: Easy

The $19/month plan caps you at 20,000 credits, which is roughly 2 hours of audio a month.

Official site Details

Fliki

Free Easy Company-made

Turns a script, blog post or slides into a video with a realistic AI voice, stock footage and music.

Price: Free (Standard plan $28/month, billed monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan makes 720p videos with a Fliki watermark and gives only 3 credits a month. Standard gives 180 credits a month.
