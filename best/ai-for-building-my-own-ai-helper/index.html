Which AI tool can build my own AI helper?

Last full review: 2026-10-10

An AI agent is a helper that does a job for you. MindStudio lets you build and run your own agents, and Relevance AI builds no-code agents for multi-step work, though its builder still takes real setup effort. AutoGPT builds a team of helpers from a plain-English description of the job, but its free version needs Docker setup and your own AI model keys; Taskade also makes an agent from a description, and Botpress builds a chatbot or support agent for your website, starting at $150 a month.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Botpress

Free Medium Company-made

Build an AI chatbot or support agent for your website, WhatsApp, or Slack that answers questions and can do real tasks.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $150/month
Difficulty: Medium

Plan prices don't include AI usage and extra conversation packs, which are billed on top and can add up fast.

Relevance AI

Free Medium Company-made

Builds no-code AI agents that can do multi-step work across apps, like sales research or lead follow-up.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $19/month (billed annually)
Difficulty: Medium

Everything is metered in Actions and credits, so real usage costs can climb past the base plan, and the builder still takes genuine setup effort.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-19

AutoGPT

Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

Build a team of AI helpers for research, content and sales work by describing the job in plain English. They can run on a schedule. A free version you set up and run yourself is also available.

Price: Free to run yourself (cloud Pro $50/month, 7-day free trial)
Difficulty: Medium

The cloud Pro plan needs a card to start the 7-day trial, then costs $50/month, and scheduled runs spend from a credit wallet with a cap you set for each helper. The free version you run yourself needs Docker setup and your own AI model keys.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-07

Taskade

Free Easy Company-made

Describe what you want and get an app, an AI agent or an automated workflow in one workspace. The free plan lets you clone community apps and has desktop and mobile apps.

Price: Free (Pro plan $20/month, or $10/month billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan covers 2 team members, up to 3 apps and 1 AI agent. Unlimited apps, agents and automations need the Pro plan at $20/month on monthly billing.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-09

MindStudio

Free Medium Company-made

Build and run your own AI agents, with 200+ AI models available through MindStudio so you do not need your own API keys.

Price: Free (Individual plan $20/month plus usage)
Difficulty: Medium

The free plan allows one agent and 1,000 runs a month. Unlimited agents and runs need the Individual plan at $20/month on monthly billing, and both plans add usage charges on top.
