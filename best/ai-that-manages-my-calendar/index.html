Lindy

Paid Easy Company-made

An AI teammate that handles your email, calendar, meetings, and routine office work when you tell it what to do.

Price: $29.99/user/month
Difficulty: Easy

The $50 of free credits lasts only 7 days, then paid use starts at $29.99 per user per month. Credits do not roll over, and bigger jobs use more of them.

Free Easy Company-made

Auto-scheduling your workday, habits, and meetings around your priorities.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $10/month
Difficulty: Easy

The $10 Starter price needs yearly billing and is per seat; month to month it is $12 each, so costs add up as your team grows.

OpenClaw

Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

An open-source AI assistant you run on your own Mac, Windows or Linux computer and message from WhatsApp, Telegram or other chat apps. It can organise your inbox, send emails and manage your calendar.

Price: Free (no subscription and no paid hosted version)
Difficulty: Medium

You bring the AI model it uses (hosted, subscription-backed or local), and any model charges are separate. It acts for you on your own computer, so connect only the accounts you are happy for it to use.
