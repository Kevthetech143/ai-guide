Can AI answer my customers' questions for me?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

Botpress and Typebot let you build a chatbot for your website, and Typebot needs no coding. Vapi and Retell AI answer real phone calls for your business, but both charge extra for the AI model and the voice on top of the headline price. Only Typebot is rated easy to set up; the other three take more work.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Vapi

Free Medium Company-made

Build an AI voice assistant that answers and makes real phone calls for your business.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $0.05/min (pay as you go)
Difficulty: Medium

The $0.05 a minute covers only Vapi's own fee. The AI model, voice, transcriber, and phone line cost extra, so the page's 1,000-minute example adds up to $82 to $129 a month.

Typebot

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

Build chatbots and conversational forms for your website by dragging and dropping blocks, no coding needed.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $39/month
Difficulty: Easy

Chat limits count every new conversation, so a busy site can burn through the free 200 chats a month quickly.

Botpress

Free Medium Company-made

Build an AI chatbot or support agent for your website, WhatsApp, or Slack that answers questions and can do real tasks.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $150/month (billed annually)
Difficulty: Medium

The $150/month price needs a yearly plan (monthly is $189). The Plus plan includes 250 conversations a month, and extra packs cost $65 per 100.

Retell AI

Free Medium Company-made

Production phone and web voice agents with SIP telephony, no-code builder, and 20 free concurrent calls.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $0.07–$0.31/minute (Pay-as-you-go Voice AI)
Difficulty: Medium

Made for businesses building phone-answering bots. The real cost per minute depends on the parts you pick: Retell's voice engine is $0.055/minute, plus the AI model and the voice (Retell's own voices add $0.015/minute).
