Can I make AI music on my own computer?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

MusicGen and ACE-Step make songs from a text description and run on your own computer. Ultimate Vocal Remover and StemRoller split a song into vocals and instruments. Many of these need some setup, so check each card first.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

MusicGen

Free Hard Open source Runs on your computer

Meta's open music-generation model you can download and run on your own computer - describe a style and it composes a matching track.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Hard

It is a raw model, not an app - running it locally takes some coding, and the CC-BY-NC license on the weights rules out commercial use.

ACE-Step

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

An open music model that makes full songs from a text description and lyrics on your own computer, with a simple web screen to use it.

Price: Free to download and run yourself
Difficulty: Medium

Setup is technical: you install it with Python, or use the ready-made download for Windows or Mac. It runs best with a strong graphics card and can be slow without one.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-05

Ultimate Vocal Remover

Free Easy Open source Runs on your computer

A free desktop app for Windows, Mac and Linux that splits a song into vocals and instruments using AI models, for karaoke tracks and remixing.

Price: Free (donations welcome)
Difficulty: Easy

Splitting runs on your own computer, so it can be slow without a good graphics card. You download the AI models you want to use.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-05

Spleeter

Free Hard Open source Runs on your computer

A free tool from Deezer that splits a song into 2, 4 or 5 parts such as vocals, drums and bass, using ready-trained AI models.

Price: Free to run yourself
Difficulty: Hard

It is a Python command-line tool with no app window, so it suits people comfortable installing software.

YuE2

Free Hard Open source Runs on your computer

An open music model that turns your lyrics and a style description into a full song with vocals and backing music. The model files are free to download and run on your own computer.

Price: Free to run yourself
Difficulty: Hard

Running it yourself needs a Linux computer with an NVIDIA graphics card with 24 GB of memory. Companies need a separate commercial license for the model files.

DiffRhythm 2

Free Hard Open source Runs on your computer

An open model that writes full-length songs with vocals that follow your lyrics. The model files are free to download and run on your own computer.

Price: Free to run yourself (free online demo available)
Difficulty: Hard

Running it yourself takes command-line steps and Python, so it suits technical users. You can try it free in your web browser on its Hugging Face demo page.

StemRoller

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

Type a song name and it splits the song into vocals, drums, bass and everything else, plus an instrumental. Handy for making karaoke tracks.

Price: Free (donations welcome)
Difficulty: Easy

Splitting a song takes several minutes. You download the app and it runs on your own computer.

Basic Pitch

Free Easy Open source Runs on your computer

Drop in a recording of one instrument, or sing into your microphone, and get a MIDI file back that you can edit in music software. It is a free tool from Spotify.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

The online tool takes a recording of a single instrument or your singing, and gives you a MIDI file to clean up, not finished sheet music. There is also a version for programmers that you install with pip.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-07

Magenta RealTime 2

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

A free music model you play like an instrument on your Mac: hold a note or chord and it generates a matching ensemble, or type a description to make a playable sound. It comes as standalone apps and a plugin for music software.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

Real-time playing needs an Apple Silicon Mac. The small model runs on any, including MacBook Air; the larger one needs an M3 Pro or M2 Max or better. Model files download on first launch (about 450MB small, 2.5GB base).

Official site Details

WanGP

Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

One free local app for AI video, image and music with models such as Wan 2.2, LTX-2, Qwen Image, Flux and ACE-Step, using a browser interface.

Price: Free to run on your own computer
Difficulty: Medium

Its site says some models run on as little as 6 GB of video memory; others need more. The license lets you use it free but not sell WanGP itself or offer it as a paid hosted service.
