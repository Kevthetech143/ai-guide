Can AI make a video of a talking person from my script?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

Synthesia turns a typed script into a video of a lifelike AI presenter, and HeyGen does it without a camera, actors, or editing skills. D-ID can also start from a photo, and Colossyan is built for training videos.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

HeyGen

Free Easy Company-made

Turns your written text into a video with a realistic AI talking person, so you can make studio-quality videos without a camera, actors, or editing skills.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $29/month
Difficulty: Easy

You pay with credits and each video costs credits, so making lots of videos can get expensive; the free plan also only gives 3 videos a month and adds a watermark.

Synthesia

Free Easy Company-made

Type a script and get a video of a lifelike AI presenter saying it, in over 140 languages.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $14/month (billed yearly)
Difficulty: Easy

The Starter plan adds only about 12 minutes of video a month on top of the free 10, and free videos carry a Synthesia watermark, so steady publishing means a bigger plan.

Colossyan

Free Easy Company-made

Type a script and get a training-style video narrated by an AI avatar, with quizzes and course exports.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $59/month (billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The $59/month price needs a yearly plan, the free plan keeps a Colossyan watermark, and unused minutes do not roll over.

Hedra

Paid Easy Company-made

Makes AI video, images and talking avatars from a prompt, in a web studio with an AI assistant.

Price: Basic $20/month (billed monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

There is no free plan in the price table, and making videos uses paid credits. Basic gives 2,000 credits a month with slower generations; faster generation starts at $50/month on the Pro plan.

D-ID

Paid Easy Company-made

Make talking videos from a photo or one of 100+ stock avatars by typing a script. Lite includes 10 minutes of video a month and fast processing.

Price: $5.9/month (Lite)
Difficulty: Easy

Lite videos carry a D-ID watermark, and the commercial use license starts on Pro at $29/month. A free 14-day trial gives 3 minutes of video.
