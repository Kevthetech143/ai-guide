Can AI summarize a web page or long document for me?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

Brave Leo and Perplexity Comet are built into a web browser and can summarize the page you are looking at, but Leo only comes with the Brave browser, and the company behind Comet says that, depending on your settings, it records your browsing history when you use Comet signed in. Claude is good at summarizing long documents, and iAsk has an instant summarizer. Qwant can condense a web page in one click if you have a free account.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Claude

Free Easy Company-made

Great for writing help, summarizing long documents, answering coding questions, and thinking ideas through carefully.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $17/month (billed annually; $20 monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan runs out of messages fast on busy days; the Pro plan costs $20 a month when billed monthly.

Perplexity Comet

Free Easy Company-made

A web browser with an AI assistant built in that can summarize pages, compare products, and do small tasks for you.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $17/month (Perplexity Pro, billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The free version of Comet does not include the browser agent that does tasks for you; that needs a paid Perplexity plan.

Brave Leo

Free Easy Company-made

An AI assistant built into the Brave browser. Ask it questions, or have it summarize the web page, document or PDF you are looking at.

Price: Free (Leo Premium $14.99/month)
Difficulty: Easy

Leo only comes with the Brave browser. Leo Premium adds more models and higher rate limits than the free version.

Official site Details

Qwant

Free Easy Company-made

A French search engine hosted in Europe that says it does not store your search history. For some factual questions its AI shows a short Flash Answer with source links above the results, and with a free Qwant account a Summary feature condenses a web page in one click.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

Qwant says its AI features are free and unlimited. The Flash Answer only appears for certain factual searches, not every search, and may not be switched on in every region or language yet. Qwant says the AI can be wrong, so check the sources it cites.
