Can I chat with an AI on my own computer?

Last full review: 2026-10-10

LM Studio, GPT4All, and Jan are desktop apps that let you chat with an AI on your own computer. Ollama downloads models like Llama and runs them for you, and AnythingLLM lets you chat with your own documents. Chatting on your own machine takes a bit more setup than a website.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Llama

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

Free, powerful AI you can download and run on your own computer or through Meta's own apps.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

You have to set it up yourself or use someone else's app to talk to it.

Mistral

Free Easy Open source Runs on your computer

A fast, free chatbot made in Europe that answers questions, searches the web, and even makes images.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $14.99/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan has daily limits, and the paid Vibe Pro plan costs $14.99 a month.

Qwen

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

Ollama

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

A free app that downloads AI models like Llama and DeepSeek and runs them on your own computer, with no internet needed after setup.

Price: $20/month (Ollama Pro; local use free)
Difficulty: Medium

You type commands to use it, the bigger models need lots of computer memory, and the new paid Ollama Pro plan costs $20 a month.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

LM Studio

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

It's a free desktop app that runs AI models right on your computer, so everything stays private and nothing you type is sent to the cloud.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

You need a fairly strong computer (at least 8GB of memory, 16GB recommended), and downloading a good model can take a while.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

GPT4All

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

A free desktop app that downloads open AI models and chats with them entirely on your computer - nothing you type ever leaves your machine.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

It is built for developers, teams, and AI power users rather than total beginners - expect to pick and configure your own models.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-21

Jan

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

A free, open-source desktop app that runs AI chat models on your computer - like ChatGPT, but your conversations stay private on your machine.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

Bigger models are large downloads, and the online-model options connect to your own accounts with those providers.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-21

AnythingLLM

Free Easy Open source Runs on your computer

A free, open-source desktop app that turns your own documents into a private AI assistant - chat with your files and get meeting summaries, all on your device with no accounts or API keys.

Price: Free to run yourself (team cloud from $50/month)
Difficulty: Easy

The desktop app that runs on your own computer is free. Their hosted team version starts at $50 a month.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-21

Pieces

Paid Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

An app that quietly remembers what you work on across your computer so you can ask AI about it later - recaps, meeting prep, finding past work

Price: $18.99 per user/month (Pro, billed monthly; 25% off billed yearly)
Difficulty: Medium

No free plan (the 7-day trial needs a card); it captures what is on your screen every 2 seconds by default, so check its privacy settings

Vane

Free Hard Company-made Runs on your computer

Running a private Perplexity-style AI answering engine entirely on your own hardware, with cited answers and local LLMs.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Hard

You install and run it yourself (the guide recommends Docker), then connect an AI model: Ollama on your own computer, or a paid key from a provider like OpenAI, Claude or Groq.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-28

Msty Studio

Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

A desktop app for chatting with AI models, either ones that run on your own computer or online ones, with personas, saved knowledge and prompt tools in one workspace.

Price: Free (Aurum $149/year, billed annually; lifetime license $349 one-time)
Difficulty: Medium

The free plan marks some features as limited, including persona chats, crew conversations and knowledge stacks. Msty Studio Web and the advanced features need the paid Aurum plan.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-07

Open WebUI

Free Hard Company-made Runs on your computer

A free chat app for AI that you install yourself and use in your web browser. It works with AI models on your own computer (through Ollama) or online ones, and adds document chat, web search, voice and image tools.

Price: Free to run yourself (Enterprise plans are by quote)
Difficulty: Hard

You install it yourself with a typed command (pip) or Docker, then connect an AI model. Business licenses, for rebranding and dedicated support, are sold only to companies.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-07

Chatbox AI

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

One app on desktop, mobile and the web for chatting with GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek and more, either with your own API keys (paid access codes from the AI companies) or with Chatbox's own plans.

Price: Free (Lite plan $3.99/month, or $3.50/month billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan gives 10,000 compute points a day, standard models only, and Work Mode (the agent feature) as a trial only. The advanced models need the Pro plan at $19.99/month on monthly billing; Lite at $3.99/month gives a bigger point allowance.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-09

LobeHub

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

Chat with many AI models and organise your own team of AI agents in one place. The free plan refreshes its credits every month, and there are desktop apps for Mac, Windows and Linux plus iPhone and Android apps.

Price: Free (Starter plan $12.9/month, or $9.9/month billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan has 500,000 credits a month, about 70 messages on the GLM-5.3-Flash model, and 15 MB of file storage. Models such as GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 need a paid plan, starting with Starter at $12.9/month on monthly billing.
