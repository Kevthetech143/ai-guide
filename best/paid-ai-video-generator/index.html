Which AI video makers are paid only?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

Luma Dream Machine turns words or a photo into realistic video clips, and Pika makes short clips with playful effects. Pictory turns a blog post or script into a captioned video, and D-ID makes a talking video from a photo and a typed script. None of them gives you free monthly videos, so expect to pay from day one.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Pika

Paid Easy Company-made

It turns your words or photos into short, fun video clips with playful effects like melting or exploding objects.

Price: $8/month (billed annually; $10 monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan gives you no monthly credits, so you buy credit packs to make clips; the cheapest plan (Starter) costs $10 a month, or $8 if you pay for a year.

Luma Dream Machine

Paid Easy Company-made

It turns a written description or a photo into a realistic short video clip, like making a still photo move or describing a scene and watching it come to life.

Price: $30/month
Difficulty: Easy

Clips are short (about 5 to 10 seconds each), and the paid Plus plan costs $30 a month, so you likely need to pay before you can create anything.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-22

Pictory

Paid Easy Company-made

Turn a blog post, script, or long recording into a captioned video with stock footage matched to your words.

Price: $25/month (billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

There is no ongoing free plan, only a 14-day trial that exports at 720p, and paying month to month costs $29 instead of $25.

Official site Details

Replicate

Paid Hard Company-made

Trying thousands of AI models (images, video, speech, music, chat) from one account, in a browser playground or through code

Price: Pay as you go per model - e.g. $0.025 per image (flux-dev), $0.04 per image (flux-1.1-pro)
Difficulty: Hard

Built for developers and billed per use - every run costs money, with no flat monthly plan

Makes AI video, images and talking avatars from a prompt, in a web studio with an AI assistant.

Difficulty: Easy

There is no free plan in the price table, and making videos uses paid credits. Basic gives 2,000 credits a month with slower generations; faster generation starts at $50/month on the Pro plan.

Submagic

Paid Easy Company-made

Upload a short video and it adds captions automatically, plus free extra clips and sound, and lets you cut the video by editing its text.

Price: $19/month
Difficulty: Easy

The $19/month Starter plan includes 45 credits (about 15 videos) a month and videos up to 2 minutes long. Automatic removal of silences and bad takes needs Pro ($39/month).

DomoAI

Paid Easy Company-made

Turn text or pictures into short AI videos and images. The Basic plan includes character-to-video, talking avatars, 4K video upscaling and watermark-free results.

Price: $13/month (Basic)
Difficulty: Easy

Basic includes 600 credits a month, which the page says is about 85 videos or 150 images. Unlimited "Relax" generations on supported models start with the Standard plan at $42/month.

Magnific

Paid Medium Company-made

One subscription for AI image, video, music and voice generation with many leading models, pro editing tools, a shared canvas for workflows called Spaces, and a stock library with a commercial AI license.

Price: $20/month (Premium; $14.50/month billed annually)
Difficulty: Medium

Monthly Premium includes 20,000 credits a month. Unlimited generations need Premium+ at $45/month ($33.75/month billed annually). Prices exclude VAT and local taxes.

D-ID

Paid Easy Company-made

Make talking videos from a photo or one of 100+ stock avatars by typing a script. Lite includes 10 minutes of video a month and fast processing.

Price: $5.9/month (Lite)
Difficulty: Easy

Lite videos carry a D-ID watermark, and the commercial use license starts on Pro at $29/month. A free 14-day trial gives 3 minutes of video.
