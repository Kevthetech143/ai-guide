Can I use AI inside WhatsApp?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

Meta AI is free and already lives inside WhatsApp, but Meta says your chats with its AI can be used to train its AI, so avoid sharing private details. OpenClaw and Hermes Agent are assistants you install on your own computer and message from WhatsApp, and both need more setup. Botpress is for businesses that want to put their own AI chatbot on WhatsApp.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Meta AI

Free Easy Company-made

It's free and already lives inside WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger, so you can ask it things right where you already chat with people.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

Meta says it trains its AI partly on what people share on its apps, including your chats with its AI features, so avoid sharing private details with it.

Botpress

Free Medium Company-made

Build an AI chatbot or support agent for your website, WhatsApp, or Slack that answers questions and can do real tasks.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $150/month (billed annually)
Difficulty: Medium

The $150/month price needs a yearly plan (monthly is $189). The Plus plan includes 250 conversations a month, and extra packs cost $65 per 100.

Hermes Agent

Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

An open-source personal AI agent you install on a Mac, Windows or Linux computer and talk to from Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, Signal or email. It remembers your projects, runs scheduled tasks and can browse the web.

Price: Free (Nous Portal Plus plan $20/month)
Difficulty: Medium

The free Nous Portal plan includes free AI models only and $0 of monthly credits. Access to 200+ models and hosted tools starts with the Plus plan at $20/month.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-09

OpenClaw

Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

An open-source AI assistant you run on your own Mac, Windows or Linux computer and message from WhatsApp, Telegram or other chat apps. It can organise your inbox, send emails and manage your calendar.

Price: Free (no subscription and no paid hosted version)
Difficulty: Medium

You bring the AI model it uses (hosted, subscription-backed or local), and any model charges are separate. It acts for you on your own computer, so connect only the accounts you are happy for it to use.
