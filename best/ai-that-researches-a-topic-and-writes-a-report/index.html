Can AI research a topic and write a report for me?

Last full review: 2026-10-10

Manus researches a topic on its own and builds a report, but its own listing says to double-check its work. Liner and Komo both offer deep research, and Komo's needs a paid plan. GPT Researcher and Local Deep Research write cited reports, but you set them up yourself.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Perplexity

Free Easy Company-made

Great for looking things up and getting answers that show exactly which websites the facts came from.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $17/month (billed annually; $20 monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan only gives you a few deep searches each day; unlimited searches cost $20 a month.

Manus

Free Medium Company-made

An AI helper that does multi-step jobs for you on its own, like researching a topic and building a report or a website.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $20/month
Difficulty: Medium

It spends credits on every task, so long jobs can burn through your plan quickly; always double-check its work.

Last checked 2026-09-14

GPT Researcher

Free Hard Company-made Runs on your computer

Self-hosted autonomous deep-research agents that aggregate 20+ web sources into cited reports on your own machine.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Hard

You must bring your own paid AI and search accounts (for example OpenAI and Tavily), and each deep research run takes about 5 minutes.

Last checked 2026-09-28

Komo

Free Easy Company-made

Asking questions and getting AI answers with links to the sources; paid plans add deep research and top AI models

Price: Free (Komo Pro $20/month, or $17/month billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan gives 25 credits a day and only Komo's fast and budget AI models. Deep research, people search and top models like GPT, Claude and Gemini need Komo Pro ($20/month for 2,000 credits)

Last checked 2026-09-30

Liner

Free Easy Company-made

An AI research helper that searches the web and academic papers, and writes answers with sources. Also has deep research reports and file uploads.

Price: Free (Pro $17.99/month, or $14.99/month billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan shows ads in answers and gives 100 credits a day and 1 file upload a day. A deep research request costs 40 credits.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-06

Local Deep Research

Free Hard Company-made Runs on your computer

An open-source research assistant you run yourself: it searches the web and your own documents with the AI model you choose, then writes a report with citations.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Hard

Setup means installing Docker (a free tool that runs apps in sealed boxes) and pasting a command from the README, which also starts a local AI model and a private search tool. Web searches still go out to the internet, and if you connect a cloud AI model instead of a local one, your questions go to that provider.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-08

Fellou

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

A web browser with an AI agent built in that carries out online jobs for you, such as research, comparing prices and putting together reports, and can run scheduled tasks.

Price: Free (Plus plan $19/month, or $16/month billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan gives a one-time bonus of 1,000 Sparks (the browser's task credits), about 4 tasks, and 2 scheduled tasks. Plus at $19/month on monthly billing gives 2,000 Sparks a month, about 8 tasks. Actual use varies with how complex the task is.
