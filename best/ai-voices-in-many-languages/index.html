Can AI speak in other languages?

231 of 231 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: October 9, 2026.

Narakeet turns a written script into a voiceover in hundreds of voices and languages, and Fish Audio does text-to-speech in 30+ languages. Synthesia is different: it makes a video of an AI presenter saying your script in over 140 languages. Free Narakeet and Fish Audio accounts are not for business use, so check before you sell or monetize what you make.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Synthesia

Free Easy Company-made

Type a script and get a video of a lifelike AI presenter saying it, in over 140 languages.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $14/month (billed yearly)
Difficulty: Easy

The Starter plan adds only about 12 minutes of video a month on top of the free 10, and free videos carry a Synthesia watermark, so steady publishing means a bigger plan.

Narakeet

Free Easy Company-made

Turns a written script into a spoken voiceover or narrated video in hundreds of voices and languages.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $6 (one-time, 30 minutes)
Difficulty: Easy

Free accounts cannot be used for commercial projects, and every full build (including re-doing one) eats your paid minutes.

Fish Audio

Free Easy Open source Runs on your computer

Natural-sounding text-to-speech and voice cloning from as little as 10 seconds of audio, in 30+ languages; the company also shares downloadable models you can run yourself.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $15/month (Plus)
Difficulty: Easy

Free Tier is for personal use only; monetizing content requires a paid plan, and unused credits do not roll over.
