Can AI speak in other languages?

Last full review: 2026-10-10

PlayAI offers over 600 voices in 142 languages and accents, and Narakeet turns a written script into a voiceover in hundreds of voices and languages. Fish Audio does text-to-speech in 30+ languages, and Synthesia is different: it makes a video of an AI presenter saying your script in over 160 languages. PlayAI has no free plan, and free Narakeet and Fish Audio accounts are not for business use, so check before you sell or monetize what you make.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

PlayAI (formerly PlayHT)

Paid Easy Company-made

It turns your written text into realistic spoken audio using over 600 voices in 142 languages and accents.

Price: No monthly price listed — check the provider's site
Difficulty: Easy

It is priced by how much audio you make, so heavy use or voice cloning can get expensive fast.

Synthesia

Free Easy Company-made

Type a script and get a video of a lifelike AI presenter saying it, in over 160 languages.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $29/month
Difficulty: Easy

The $29 Starter plan covers only about 120 minutes of finished video for the whole year, so steady publishing means a bigger plan.

Narakeet

Free Easy Company-made

Turns a written script into a spoken voiceover or narrated video in hundreds of voices and languages.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $6 (one-time, 30 minutes)
Difficulty: Easy

Free accounts cannot be used for commercial projects, and every full build (including re-doing one) eats your paid minutes.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-19

Fish Audio

Free Easy Open source Runs on your computer

Natural-sounding text-to-speech and voice cloning from as little as 10 seconds of audio, in 30+ languages; the company also shares downloadable models you can run yourself.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $15/month (Plus)
Difficulty: Easy

Free Tier is for personal use only; monetizing content requires a paid plan, and unused credits do not roll over.
