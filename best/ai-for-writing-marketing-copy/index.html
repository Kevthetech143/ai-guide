Is there an AI that writes marketing copy?

Last full review: 2026-10-10

Copy.ai writes marketing copy, emails, and social posts in your brand voice. Jasper writes on-brand marketing content but is built for teams rather than casual use. Rytr is a budget pick for short marketing copy and emails, and ChatSonic now lives inside Writesonic's marketing platform.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

ChatSonic

Free Easy Company-made

Drafting marketing content and pulling live web data inside one chat window.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $16/month
Difficulty: Easy

It is now folded into Writesonic's marketing platform, and the $16/month price is billed annually.
