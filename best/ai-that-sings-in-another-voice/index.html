Can AI sing my song in a different voice?

Last full review: 2026-10-10

Jammable makes AI cover songs by singing your track in a voice you pick, and Audimee turns a vocal recording into a different singing voice. Kits AI trains AI voices to sing for your tracks, and ACE Studio makes AI singing voices from your melody and lyrics. Jammable and ACE Studio have no free plan, and many of Jammable's voices imitate real singers, so check you are allowed to share what you make.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Kits AI

Free Easy Company-made

Training AI voices to sing or speak for your music tracks.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $10/month
Difficulty: Easy

The cheapest paid plan is Starter at $10 a month, and it limits how many custom voices you can train.

ACE Studio

Paid Medium Company-made

AI singing voices from your melody and lyrics, voice cloning, and AI instruments that work alongside music-making software.

Price: $20.73/month Artist (billed annually)
Difficulty: Medium

Generative kits, stem splitter, and video tools are metered from a 2,500 credits/month pool on the Artist plan.

Jammable

Paid Easy Company-made

Make AI cover songs: pick a voice from a library of 100,000+ voices, add your track and it sings it in that voice. It also has a voice changer and text to speech.

Price: $8.99/month (no free plan)
Difficulty: Easy

There is no free plan; the cheapest plan is Basic at $8.99 a month. Many voices imitate real singers and characters, so check you are allowed to share what you make.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-06

Audimee

Free Easy Company-made

Turn a vocal recording into a different singing voice, split a song into vocals and instruments, and make harmonies. It is aimed at music makers.

Price: Free (Starter $12/month)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan gives 15 minutes of conversions, MP3 downloads only and personal use only, with no harmony maker, and you cannot save your conversions. Starter includes 1 hour of conversions a month.
