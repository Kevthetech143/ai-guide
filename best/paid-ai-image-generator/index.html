Which AI picture makers are paid only?

Last full review: 2026-10-10

Midjourney makes beautiful art from a typed description. Photoshop's generative fill adds or removes things in a photo from a text prompt, and Topaz Photo sharpens and cleans up photos. Magnific bundles image, video, music, and voice tools in one subscription. None of them has a free plan, so expect to pay from day one.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Midjourney

Paid Easy Company-made

Beautiful AI pictures and art that look great, made by typing a simple description.

Price: $10/month
Difficulty: Easy

There is no free plan and your pictures are public unless you pay more.

Photoshop (generative fill)

Paid Medium Company-made

Inside Photoshop, add or remove anything in a photo with a text prompt - the AI fills in the rest to match.

Price: $9.99/month (with Photoshop)
Difficulty: Medium

You need a paid Photoshop or Creative Cloud plan, and it takes some practice to use well.

Official site Details

Getimg.ai

Paid Easy Company-made

Generating and editing AI images for art, thumbnails, and design mockups.

Price: $8/month (billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The $8/month Entry plan is billed yearly ($96 upfront), plan credits do not roll over, and API access costs extra.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-20

Replicate

Paid Hard Company-made

Trying thousands of AI models (images, video, speech, music, chat) from one account, in a browser playground or through code

Price: Pay as you go per model - e.g. $0.025 per image (flux-dev), $0.04 per image (flux-1.1-pro)
Difficulty: Hard

Built for developers and billed per use - every run costs money, with no flat monthly plan

Topaz Photo

Paid Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

AI photo enhancement: denoise, sharpen, upscale, face recovery.

Price: $39/month (Personal, billed monthly), or $199/year
Difficulty: Easy

Commercial use limited to orgs under $1M USD annual revenue.

Pixlr

Paid Easy Company-made

Edit photos and make AI images in your web browser. The Plus plan removes ads, allows unlimited saves and includes 80 AI credits each month.

Price: $2.49/month (Plus)
Difficulty: Easy

Plus's 80 monthly credits cover about 80 fast AI images or about 16 higher-quality ones. Unlimited fast image generations (fair use applies) need the Ultra plan, which starts at $24.99/month.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-08

DomoAI

Paid Easy Company-made

Turn text or pictures into short AI videos and images. The Basic plan includes character-to-video, talking avatars, 4K video upscaling and watermark-free results.

Price: $13/month (Basic)
Difficulty: Easy

Basic includes 600 credits a month, which the page says is about 85 videos or 150 images. Unlimited "Relax" generations on supported models start with the Standard plan at $42/month.

Official site Details

Magnific

Paid Medium Company-made

One subscription for AI image, video, music and voice generation with many leading models, pro editing tools, a shared canvas for workflows called Spaces, and a stock library with a commercial AI license.

Price: $20/month (Premium; $14.50/month billed annually)
Difficulty: Medium

Monthly Premium includes 20,000 credits a month. Unlimited generations need Premium+ at $45/month ($33.75/month billed annually). Prices exclude VAT and local taxes.

Stockimg AI

Paid Easy Company-made

Generate logos, posters, illustrations, wallpapers and stock-style pictures from a text description. Starter also includes AI background removal and 4x upscaling.

Price: $12/month (Starter)
Difficulty: Easy

Starter includes 300 credits a month. Unlimited credits need the Ultra plan at $48/month.

Mage

Paid Easy Company-made

Make AI images in your browser with unlimited generations on base models such as Stable Diffusion and Flux 2, plus add-on styles (LoRAs) and inpainting (redrawing part of a picture).

Price: $10/month (Basic)
Difficulty: Easy

Basic includes 500 Gems a month and 2x speed. Unlimited video generation and ControlNet (pose and layout control) need Pro at $30/month.
