Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Leonardo AI

Free Medium Company-made

Great for game art, character designs, and keeping the same art style across many pictures.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $12/month
Difficulty: Medium

It uses tokens instead of a simple price, so free users only get 150 tokens a day, and keeping pictures private needs a paid plan.

Draw Things

Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

A free image-making app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that creates pictures from text on your own device, offline. It can also train your own custom styles on the device.

Price: Free (Draw Things+ $8.99/month for more cloud power)
Difficulty: Medium

It is built for Apple devices. Cloud compute needs a free account, and higher cloud limits and custom style uploads need Draw Things+ at $8.99 a month.

Scenario

Free Medium Company-made

A workspace for making game, product and brand art with 500+ AI models. The free tier gives 50 credits a day with no credit card; Starter includes 1,500 credits a month and private generations.

Price: Free (Starter $15/month)
Difficulty: Medium

Training your own custom models, unlimited relaxed generations and the priority queue need Pro at $45/month.
