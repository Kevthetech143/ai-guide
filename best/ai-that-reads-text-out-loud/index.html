Can AI read text out loud to me?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

Speechify reads articles, documents, and books aloud, but its free voices sound robotic and the natural-voice plan costs $29 a month. Typecast lets you paste text and pick a voice to read it, and ElevenLabs turns written words into natural spoken audio. Kokoro and Piper run on your own computer but need technical setup, so they suit tinkerers.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

ElevenLabs

Free Easy Company-made

It turns your written words into natural-sounding spoken audio, great for videos, podcasts, or audiobooks.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $5/month (billed annually; $6 monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan is small, so you will hit the monthly limit quickly if you make lots of audio.

Speechify

Free Easy Company-made

Listening to articles, documents, and books read aloud in natural-sounding voices.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $29/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan's voices sound robotic, and the natural-voice Premium plan costs $29/month.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-24

Kokoro

Free Hard Open source Runs on your computer

A small, fast open text-to-speech model that reads text aloud in a natural voice. Light enough to run on an ordinary computer.

Price: Free to run yourself
Difficulty: Hard

It is a model, not an app, so you need some Python skills or a tool built on top of it. Check the model page for which languages and voices it covers.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-06

Piper

Free Hard Open source Runs on your computer

A fast text-to-speech engine that runs fully on your own computer, with many free downloadable voices. Good for reading text aloud without sending it to the cloud.

Price: Free to run yourself
Difficulty: Hard

You install it from the command line, so it suits technical users. The project says it is looking for new maintainers.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-06

Typecast

Free Easy Company-made

Type or paste a script and pick an AI voice to read it aloud, with video export. Voice generation and playback are free; you spend credits when you download.

Price: Free (Basic $5/month)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan allows about 5 minutes of audio downloads in total and requires you to credit Typecast on what you download. Basic includes about 35 minutes of downloads each month.
