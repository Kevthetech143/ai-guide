Can AI help me make short videos for TikTok and Reels?

231 of 231 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: October 9, 2026.

Opus Clip turns a long video or podcast into short vertical clips with captions for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. CapCut AI is a free editor from the maker of TikTok, and Submagic adds captions to a short video you already filmed.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

CapCut AI

Free Easy Company-made

A free video editor from the maker of TikTok with AI captions, background removal, and text-to-speech built right in.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $19.99/month
Difficulty: Easy

Some features and cloud storage need the paid Pro plan, which costs $19.99 a month; CapCut is best for short videos, not full movies.

Opus Clip

Free Easy Company-made

Turn a long video or podcast into short vertical clips with captions for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $15/month
Difficulty: Easy

You pay per minute of video you upload, not per clip you get, so long source videos burn through the plan fast.

Wisecut

Free Easy Company-made

Automatically cuts silences, adds subtitles and background music, and turns long videos into short clips.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from US$189/year
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan exports only 480p clips up to 3 minutes long. The Starter+ plan gives 300 credits a month, and each minute of editing uses one, so long videos use them up fast.

Lumen5

Free Easy Company-made

Turns a blog post, link or script into a short social video with AI help. Built mainly for business use.

Price: Free (Basic plan $29/month, billed monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan allows 5 videos a month, up to 2 minutes each, with a Lumen5 branded outro. The Basic plan removes the branding.

Submagic

Paid Easy Company-made

Upload a short video and it adds captions automatically, plus free extra clips and sound, and lets you cut the video by editing its text.

Price: $19/month
Difficulty: Easy

The $19/month Starter plan includes 45 credits (about 15 videos) a month and videos up to 2 minutes long. Automatic removal of silences and bad takes needs Pro ($39/month).

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-30

Viggle

Free Easy Company-made

Swap any character into trending clips, or turn a phone video into motion for your own character, to make memes and short social videos. The free plan includes 5 videos a day.

Price: Free (Pro $9.99/month)
Difficulty: Easy

Free allows one video at a time and keeps your files for 7 days. Pro at $9.99/month adds 80 credits a month, no watermark and permanent storage; access to all models needs the Live plan at $19.99/month.
