Can AI write and sing a song for me?

231 of 231 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: October 9, 2026.

Suno and Udio turn a few words into a full song with singing, and Udio's songs come with lyrics you can tweak. Flow Music lets you chat with an AI co-producer to make full songs with vocals, and Mureka and MusicGPT also make songs from typed words. Songs made on Suno's free plan cannot be used for business, and Udio's terms say its songs are for personal use only, not business.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Udio

Free Easy Company-made

It turns a short description into a full song with music, singing, and lyrics that you can tweak and extend.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $8/month (billed annually; $10 monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan gives you 100 credits a month, no more than 10 a day, and only 3 full-length songs a day; the cheapest paid plan (Standard) costs $10 a month, or $8 if you pay for a year.
