Can AI do a job for me on a schedule?

Last full review: 2026-10-10

Gumloop builds AI automations that run on a schedule, and AutoGPT helpers can run on a schedule too. Fellou is a web browser with an AI agent that can run scheduled tasks, and its free plan includes 2 of them. Hermes Agent is an assistant you install on your own computer that runs scheduled tasks and can browse the web, so it needs more setup; Gumloop has no free plan, and the free AutoGPT version also needs technical setup.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Gumloop

Paid Easy Company-made

Building no-code AI automations that scrape sites, process data, and run on a schedule.

Price: $37/month
Difficulty: Easy

Pro starts at $37/month and usage is credit-based, so large workflows can cost more than the base price.

AutoGPT

Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

Build a team of AI helpers for research, content and sales work by describing the job in plain English. They can run on a schedule. A free version you set up and run yourself is also available.

Price: Free to run yourself (cloud Pro $50/month, 7-day free trial)
Difficulty: Medium

The cloud Pro plan needs a card to start the 7-day trial, then costs $50/month, and scheduled runs spend from a credit wallet with a cap you set for each helper. The free version you run yourself needs Docker setup and your own AI model keys.

Last checked 2026-10-07

Fellou

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

A web browser with an AI agent built in that carries out online jobs for you, such as research, comparing prices and putting together reports, and can run scheduled tasks.

Price: Free (Plus plan $19/month, or $16/month billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan gives a one-time bonus of 1,000 Sparks (the browser's task credits), about 4 tasks, and 2 scheduled tasks. Plus at $19/month on monthly billing gives 2,000 Sparks a month, about 8 tasks. Actual use varies with how complex the task is.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-09

Hermes Agent

Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

An open-source personal AI agent you install on a Mac, Windows or Linux computer and talk to from Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, Signal or email. It remembers your projects, runs scheduled tasks and can browse the web.

Price: Free (Nous Portal Plus plan $20/month)
Difficulty: Medium

The free Nous Portal plan includes free AI models only and $0 of monthly credits. Access to 200+ models and hosted tools starts with the Plus plan at $20/month.
