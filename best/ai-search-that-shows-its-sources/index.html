Is there an AI search that shows its sources?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

Perplexity answers your question and shows exactly which websites the facts came from, and Scira, Morphic, Liner and Komo give an AI answer with its sources listed. Brave Search puts AI answers that cite their sources above its results, Qwant sometimes shows a short answer with source links, and Andi gives conversational answers, though its own terms say the AI can make mistakes. Several free plans cap how many searches you get a day, Scira's at just 3, so whichever you use, click a source before you trust the answer.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Perplexity

Free Easy Company-made

Great for looking things up and getting answers that show exactly which websites the facts came from.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $17/month (billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan is only good for limited daily use; the cheapest paid plan (Pro) costs $17 a month when you pay for a year.

Andi

Free Easy Company-made

Ad-free conversational answers and visual search results with a privacy-first, no-tracking positioning.

Price: Free (a separate Search API for developers is paid per query)
Difficulty: Easy

AI answers can contain mistakes (its terms say so), so check the sources it shows. Only the search site is free; the developer Search API is paid.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-28

Komo

Free Easy Company-made

Asking questions and getting AI answers with links to the sources; paid plans add deep research and top AI models

Price: Free (Komo Pro $20/month, or $17/month billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan gives 25 credits a day and only Komo's fast and budget AI models. Deep research, people search and top models like GPT, Claude and Gemini need Komo Pro ($20/month for 2,000 credits)

Last checked 2026-09-30

Scira

Free Easy Company-made

An AI search engine that answers with sources from the web, Reddit, X, YouTube and academic papers. The code is open source.

Price: Free (3 searches a day); Pro $15/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan allows only 3 searches a day. Pro has unlimited searches, but some top models have monthly or weekly caps.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-05

Liner

Free Easy Company-made

An AI research helper that searches the web and academic papers, and writes answers with sources. Also has deep research reports and file uploads.

Price: Free (Pro $17.99/month, or $14.99/month billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan shows ads in answers and gives 100 credits a day and 1 file upload a day. A deep research request costs 40 credits.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-06

Morphic

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

A free AI search engine that gives a complete answer with its sources instead of a list of links. Use it on its website, or run your own copy, because the code is open source.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

Running your own copy takes technical setup with Docker plus at least one AI provider key (OpenAI, Anthropic or Google, for example), and paid keys have their own costs.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-07

Brave Search (Ask Brave)

Free Easy Company-made

A private search engine built on its own index. It puts AI answers that cite their sources at the top of results, and Ask Brave gives a chat-style answer with supporting websites and videos, then lets you ask follow-up questions.

Price: Free (ad-free Search Premium $3/month, or $2.50/month billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

Free searches show private ads. Search Premium, the paid ad-free option, is only available in the Brave browser.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-10

Qwant

Free Easy Company-made

A French search engine hosted in Europe that says it does not store your search history. For some factual questions its AI shows a short Flash Answer with source links above the results, and with a free Qwant account a Summary feature condenses a web page in one click.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

Qwant says its AI features are free and unlimited. The Flash Answer only appears for certain factual searches, not every search, and may not be switched on in every region or language yet. Qwant says the AI can be wrong, so check the sources it cites.
