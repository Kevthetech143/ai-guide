Which AI is fun to play with?

231 of 231 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: October 9, 2026.

Pika turns your words or photos into short, fun video clips with playful effects like melting or exploding objects, though you have to buy credits to make them. Viggle swaps any character into trending clips to make memes. Character AI is great for chatting and roleplaying with fun characters, and Grok is great for fun chats and is free to try, though you need to sign in with an X or email account.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Grok

Free Easy Company-made

Great for fun chats, quick questions, seeing what's happening on X right now, and making pictures from words.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $8/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan has limited use; the cheapest paid plan (Plus) costs $8 a month, and the Premium plan costs $30 a month.

Pika

Paid Easy Company-made

It turns your words or photos into short, fun video clips with playful effects like melting or exploding objects.

Price: $8/month (billed annually; $10 monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan gives you no monthly credits, so you buy credit packs to make clips; the cheapest plan (Starter) costs $10 a month, or $8 if you pay for a year.

Character AI

Free Easy Company-made

Great for chatting and roleplaying with fun characters you pick or create yourself - story heroes, historical figures, and helpful personas.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $4.99/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan shows ads and has some limits; the cheapest paid plan (lite) starts at $4.99 a month, and the price changes by country.

Viggle

Free Easy Company-made

Swap any character into trending clips, or turn a phone video into motion for your own character, to make memes and short social videos. The free plan includes 5 videos a day.

Price: Free (Pro $9.99/month)
Difficulty: Easy

Free allows one video at a time and keeps your files for 7 days. Pro at $9.99/month adds 80 credits a month, no watermark and permanent storage; access to all models needs the Live plan at $19.99/month.
