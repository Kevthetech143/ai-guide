Is there an AI that types what I say?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

Handy is a free app: hold a key, speak, and your words are typed into whatever box you are using. Buzz and Vibe are free apps that turn audio and video files into written text on your own computer. Fish Audio goes the other way and reads text out loud.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Fish Audio

Free Easy Open source Runs on your computer

Natural-sounding text-to-speech and voice cloning from as little as 10 seconds of audio, in 30+ languages; the company also shares downloadable models you can run yourself.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $15/month (Plus)
Difficulty: Easy

Free Tier is for personal use only; monetizing content requires a paid plan, and unused credits do not roll over.

Buzz

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

A free app that turns audio and video into text, and translates it, entirely on your own computer using OpenAI's Whisper. Nothing has to be uploaded.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

The latest Mac version needs an Apple silicon Mac (the last Intel Mac version is 1.4.5). The Windows installer is not signed, so you will see a warning when you install it.

Vibe

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

A free app that transcribes audio and video files into text on your own device, with subtitles export and speaker labels. Works on Mac, Windows and Linux.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

Transcription speed depends on your computer. Optional summaries use the Claude API or Ollama, which you set up separately.

Handy

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

A free dictation app: hold a shortcut key, speak, and your words are typed into whatever text box you are using. It works on your own computer without sending audio to the cloud.

Price: Free (donations welcome)
Difficulty: Easy

It does one thing: turns your speech into text in the box you are typing in.
