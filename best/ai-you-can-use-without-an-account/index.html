Which AI can I try without making an account?

231 of 231 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: October 9, 2026.

Lumo is a private AI chat you can try without an account, and a free account lets you keep your chat history. Ecosia lets you try its AI Chat without signing in, but its full free features need a free account. AnythingLLM is a free desktop app that works with no accounts or API keys, and Dyad needs no sign-up to download, but to use it you bring your own AI access, such as a free local model or a paid key.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

AnythingLLM

Free Easy Open source Runs on your computer

A free, open-source desktop app that turns your own documents into a private AI assistant - chat with your files and get meeting summaries, all on your device with no accounts or API keys.

Price: Free to run yourself (team cloud from $50/month)
Difficulty: Easy

The desktop app that runs on your own computer is free. Their hosted team version starts at $50 a month.

Lumo

Free Easy Company-made

A private AI chat assistant from Proton, the email and privacy company. You can try it without an account, and a free account lets you keep your chat history.

Price: Free (Lumo Plus $12.99/month)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan limits use of the Max model, messages and chat history, and image generations, and allows 1 project. Lumo Plus raises those limits.

Dyad

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

A free desktop app that turns a plain-English description into a working web app on your own computer, using the AI model you choose. No sign-up is needed to download it.

Price: Free (Pro plan $20/month)
Difficulty: Easy

You bring your own AI access, such as an API key (a paid access code from an AI company), a ChatGPT subscription or a free local model. The free plan's built-in agent mode is limited to 20 messages a day; the fuller Pro Agent mode with its own AI credits comes with Pro at $20/month.

Ecosia

Free Easy Company-made

A search engine that uses all its profits for climate action and has an AI Chat tab next to web results. Ecosia says its small AI models give accurate answers from reliable sources and that your chats are never used to train AI models.

Price: Free (AI Chat Starter plan $7.99/month, Pro plan $14.99/month)
Difficulty: Easy

You can try AI Chat without signing in, but the free plan's full features need a free Ecosia account and have daily limits on file uploads and Think longer mode. Unlimited AI Chat messages need the Starter plan at $7.99/month.
