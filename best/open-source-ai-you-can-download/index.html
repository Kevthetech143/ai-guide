What open-source AI can I download?

Llama, Mistral, Qwen, Stable Diffusion, Flux, Whisper, and Gemma are free to download and use, with code anyone can inspect. They're the reason AI can run privately on your own computer.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Llama

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

Free, powerful AI you can download and run on your own computer or through Meta's own apps.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

You have to set it up yourself or use someone else's app to talk to it.

Free Easy Open source Runs on your computer

A fast, free chatbot made in Europe that answers questions, searches the web, and even makes images.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $14.99/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan has daily limits, and the paid Vibe Pro plan costs $14.99 a month.

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

It makes very detailed pictures from a short written description, like posters, art, or product mockups.

Difficulty: Medium

Whisper

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

It turns spoken audio into written text, great for transcribing meetings, lectures, or video subtitles. It understands dozens of languages.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

You have to install it yourself with some command-line steps, and it needs a decent computer to run well.

Ollama

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

A free app that downloads AI models like Llama and DeepSeek and runs them on your own computer, with no internet needed after setup.

Price: $20/month (Ollama Pro; local use free)
Difficulty: Medium

You type commands to use it, the bigger models need lots of computer memory, and the new paid Ollama Pro plan costs $20 a month.

Gemma

Free Hard Open source

It's a free, openly available AI model from Google (built with the same research as Gemini) that developers can download, customize, and build with.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Hard

Gemma is a raw model, not an app — you need technical tools to actually run it, so it's hard for non-technical people to use on its own.

Difficulty: Medium
