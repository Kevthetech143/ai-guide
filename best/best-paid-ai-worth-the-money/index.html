Which paid AI is actually worth the money?

Last full review: 2026-09-14

If you make images every day, Midjourney's image quality is the reason professionals pay for it. ChatGPT Plus unlocks the ChatGPT agent and Sora video, and Claude Code pays for itself if coding is part of your job.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Midjourney

Paid Easy Company-made

Beautiful AI pictures and art that look great, made by typing a simple description.

Price: $10/month
Difficulty: Easy

There is no free plan and your pictures are public unless you pay more.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

Veo

Paid Medium Company-made

Google's top video maker; it turns words or photos into sharp, cinematic video clips, even with matching sound.

Price: $20/month (Google AI Pro)
Difficulty: Medium

The cheapest official way in is the Google AI Pro plan at $20 a month; you pay per second of video, so costs add up fast.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

Claude Code

Paid Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

An AI coding helper that works in your terminal; it can read your whole project, make changes, and run tests.

Price: $17/month (billed annually; $20 monthly)
Difficulty: Medium

There is no free access, and usage limits mean heavy coding days can hit the cap.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

Luma Dream Machine

Paid Easy Company-made

It turns a written description or a photo into a realistic short video clip, like making a still photo move or describing a scene and watching it come to life.

Price: $30/month
Difficulty: Easy

Clips are short (about 5 to 10 seconds each), and the paid Plus plan costs $30 a month, so you likely need to pay before you can create anything.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

PlayAI (formerly PlayHT)

Paid Easy Company-made

It turns your written text into realistic spoken audio using over 600 voices in 142 languages and accents.

Price: No monthly price listed — check the provider's site
Difficulty: Easy

It is priced by how much audio you make, so heavy use or voice cloning can get expensive fast.

Official site Details

Photoshop (generative fill)

Paid Medium Company-made

Inside Photoshop, add or remove anything in a photo with a text prompt - the AI fills in the rest to match.

Price: $9.99/month (with Photoshop)
Difficulty: Medium

You need a paid Photoshop or Creative Cloud plan, and it takes some practice to use well.

ChatGPT agent

Paid Easy Company-made

Great for multi-step online chores: it browses the web, fills out forms, compares prices, and finishes tasks for you.

Price: $20/month (with ChatGPT Plus)
Difficulty: Easy

It needs a paid ChatGPT plan, and you should check its work before it books or buys anything.
