Which AI tools does Meta make?

231 of 231 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: October 9, 2026.

Meta AI is free and lives inside WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger, but Meta says your chats with its AI can be used to train its AI, so avoid sharing private details. Llama is free AI you can download and run on your own computer or use through Meta's own apps. MusicGen is Meta's music model for your own computer, but it takes some coding and its license rules out commercial use.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Meta AI

Free Easy Company-made

It's free and already lives inside WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger, so you can ask it things right where you already chat with people.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

Meta says it trains its AI partly on what people share on its apps, including your chats with its AI features, so avoid sharing private details with it.

MusicGen

Free Hard Open source Runs on your computer

Meta's open music-generation model you can download and run on your own computer - describe a style and it composes a matching track.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Hard

It is a raw model, not an app - running it locally takes some coding, and the CC-BY-NC license on the weights rules out commercial use.
