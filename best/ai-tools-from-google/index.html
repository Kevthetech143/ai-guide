Which AI tools does Google make?

231 of 231 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: October 9, 2026.

Gemini answers questions and helps inside Google apps like Gmail and Docs, Google AI Mode gives full AI answers with links inside Google Search, and NotebookLM answers questions from documents you upload. Veo turns words or photos into video clips, with a small free daily allowance through Google Flow, and Google Flow is Google's studio for making videos and images. Flow Music makes full songs with vocals, and Magenta RealTime 2 is a free music model you play like an instrument, though it needs a newer Mac.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Gemini

Free Easy Company-made

Great for quick answers, help inside Google apps like Gmail and Docs, and looking things up on the web.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $4.99/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free version has usage limits, and the cheapest paid plan (Google AI Plus) costs $4.99 a month.

Veo

Free Medium Company-made

Google's top video maker; it turns words or photos into sharp, cinematic video clips, even with matching sound.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $4.99/month (Google AI Plus)
Difficulty: Medium

The free plan gives you 50 Google Flow credits a day, and each video uses credits; the cheapest paid plan (Google AI Plus) costs $4.99 a month, and prices may vary by country.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-21

Google AI Mode

Free Easy Company-made

A smarter way to Google: it gives full AI answers with links, and you can ask follow-up questions.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

It is built into Google Search. You can use it without signing in, but you need to sign in and turn on history to pick up where you left off, and its answers can include mistakes.

NotebookLM

Free Easy Company-made

Great for studying: upload your documents and ask it questions, or turn them into a podcast-style audio chat between two AI hosts.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

It only answers from the documents you upload, so weak sources mean weak answers.

Flow Music

Free Easy Company-made

Chat with an AI co-producer to make full songs with vocals, then publish and share them - the free plan tops up credits daily.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $6/month (billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The $6 price needs yearly billing; the page opens on the yearly view, and month to month Starter is $8. Free gives daily top-up credits and 2 generations at a time.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-26

Magenta RealTime 2

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

A free music model you play like an instrument on your Mac: hold a note or chord and it generates a matching ensemble, or type a description to make a playable sound. It comes as standalone apps and a plugin for music software.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

Real-time playing needs an Apple Silicon Mac. The small model runs on any, including MacBook Air; the larger one needs an M3 Pro or M2 Max or better. Model files download on first launch (about 450MB small, 2.5GB base).

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-31

Google Flow

Free Easy Company-made

Google's AI studio for making videos and images: text to video, frames to video and image generation with models such as Veo and Nano Banana, free to try with a Google account.

Price: Free (paid plans from $4.99/month via Google AI Plus)
Difficulty: Easy

You must be 18 or older. The free plan gives 50 credits a day. 1080p video upscaling and higher limits start with Google AI Plus at $4.99/month; prices vary by market.
