Which AI can put a character into a video?

Last full review: 2026-10-10

Viggle swaps any character into trending clips, or turns a phone video into motion for your own character, to make memes and short social videos, with 5 free videos a day. DomoAI turns text or pictures into short videos and its Basic plan includes character-to-video, but it has no free plan and starts at $13 a month. Vidu makes short videos from reference pictures of a character or object, and its free plan is limited.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Vidu

Free Easy Company-made

Make short AI videos from text, from a photo, or from reference pictures of a character or object.

Price: Free (Standard $10/month)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan is limited, for example 10 saved references a month. Standard includes 800 credits a month, which the page says covers up to 200 videos.

DomoAI

Paid Easy Company-made

Turn text or pictures into short AI videos and images. The Basic plan includes character-to-video, talking avatars, 4K video upscaling and watermark-free results.

Price: $13/month (Basic)
Difficulty: Easy

Basic includes 600 credits a month, which the page says is about 85 videos or 150 images. Unlimited "Relax" generations on supported models start with the Standard plan at $42/month.

Viggle

Free Easy Company-made

Swap any character into trending clips, or turn a phone video into motion for your own character, to make memes and short social videos. The free plan includes 5 videos a day.

Price: Free (Pro $9.99/month)
Difficulty: Easy

Free allows one video at a time and keeps your files for 7 days. Pro at $9.99/month adds 80 credits a month, no watermark and permanent storage; access to all models needs the Live plan at $19.99/month.
