Can AI automate boring jobs between my apps?

231 of 231 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: October 9, 2026.

Zapier AI connects your apps and builds the steps from a plain sentence, like saving email attachments to Google Drive, with no coding. Gumloop and Taskade also set up automations, and Relevance AI builds no-code AI agents that do work across apps, such as sales research. n8n builds powerful automations across hundreds of apps but is built for technical users, so the first setup can feel hard; Zapier's free plan covers only 100 tasks a month, and Gumloop has no free plan.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Zapier AI

Free Easy Company-made

It connects your apps and does boring tasks for you — like saving email attachments to Google Drive — without writing any code, and an AI helper builds the workflows from a plain sentence.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $19.99/month (billed annually; $29.99 monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan covers only 100 tasks a month and caps workflows at two steps, so costs rise quickly as you automate more.

n8n

Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

It lets you build powerful step-by-step automations across hundreds of apps, and the free Community edition runs on your own machine with no limits on executions.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $20/month (billed annually; $24 monthly)
Difficulty: Medium

It's built for technical users, so the first setup can feel hard if you've never made a workflow before; the cloud plan costs $24 a month, or $20 a month if you pay for a year, or you can run it on your own computer for free.

Relevance AI

Paid Medium Company-made

Builds no-code AI agents that can do multi-step work across apps, like sales research or lead follow-up.

Price: $19/month (billed annually)
Difficulty: Medium

The free plan has been retired, so you pay from day one. Pro is $19/month on yearly billing ($29 month to month) and includes 2,500 Actions and $20 of vendor credits a month.

Gumloop

Paid Easy Company-made

Building no-code AI automations that scrape sites, process data, and run on a schedule.

Price: $37/month
Difficulty: Easy

Pro starts at $37/month and usage is credit-based, so large workflows can cost more than the base price.

Taskade

Free Easy Company-made

Describe what you want and get an app, an AI agent or an automated workflow in one workspace. The free plan lets you clone community apps and has desktop and mobile apps.

Price: Free (Starter $6/month, billed yearly only; Pro $20/month, or $10/month billed yearly)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan covers 2 people, 3 live apps and 1 AI agent. Starter ($6/month, billed yearly) gives unlimited apps and 3 agents; unlimited agents and automations need the Pro plan at $20/month on monthly billing.
