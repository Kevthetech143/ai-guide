Which AI can split a song into vocals, drums and bass?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

LALAL.AI splits any song into vocals, drums, bass and other parts in seconds, and Fadr pulls a song apart so you can remix it even with no music experience, though its free plan splits only the main parts. StemRoller and Ultimate Vocal Remover are free apps that run on your own computer, and Spleeter is free too but has no app window, so it suits people comfortable installing software. Audimee also splits songs, but it is aimed at music makers.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

LALAL.AI

Free Easy Company-made

It splits any song into vocals, drums, bass, and other stems in seconds, and cleans up noisy recordings - with desktop, phone, and DAW apps.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $7.50/month (billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

Paid plans give unlimited relaxed-queue minutes but only a fixed monthly amount of fast-queue minutes (90 on Lite, 250 on Pro); the VST plugin and local desktop processing are Pro-only.

Fadr

Free Easy Company-made

Using AI to pull a song apart into separate tracks (vocals, drums, bass, melody) and remix it, even with no music experience

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $10/month (Fadr Plus), or $100/year
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan splits songs into the main parts and gives MP3 downloads only. Finer splits (like single drums), higher-quality WAV files and the add-ons for music software need Fadr Plus

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-30

Ultimate Vocal Remover

Free Easy Open source Runs on your computer

A free desktop app for Windows, Mac and Linux that splits a song into vocals and instruments using AI models, for karaoke tracks and remixing.

Price: Free (donations welcome)
Difficulty: Easy

Splitting runs on your own computer, so it can be slow without a good graphics card. You download the AI models you want to use.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-05

Spleeter

Free Hard Open source Runs on your computer

A free tool from Deezer that splits a song into 2, 4 or 5 parts such as vocals, drums and bass, using ready-trained AI models.

Price: Free to run yourself
Difficulty: Hard

It is a Python command-line tool with no app window, so it suits people comfortable installing software.

StemRoller

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

Type a song name and it splits the song into vocals, drums, bass and everything else, plus an instrumental. Handy for making karaoke tracks.

Price: Free (donations welcome)
Difficulty: Easy

Splitting a song takes several minutes. You download the app and it runs on your own computer.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-06

Audimee

Free Easy Company-made

Turn a vocal recording into a different singing voice, split a song into vocals and instruments, and make harmonies. It is aimed at music makers.

Price: Free (Starter $12/month)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan gives 15 minutes of conversions, MP3 downloads only and personal use only, with no harmony maker, and you cannot save your conversions. Starter includes 1 hour of conversions a month.
