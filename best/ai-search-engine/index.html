Is there an AI search engine?

Last full review: 2026-09-14

Yes. Perplexity, Google AI Mode, and You.com answer questions by looking things up and showing their sources. They're free, and they feel like search with a smart assistant on top.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Perplexity

Free Easy Company-made

Great for looking things up and getting answers that show exactly which websites the facts came from.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $17/month (billed annually; $20 monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan only gives you a few deep searches each day; unlimited searches cost $20 a month.

Google AI Mode

Free Easy Company-made

A smarter way to Google: it gives full AI answers with links, and you can ask follow-up questions.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

It is built into Google Search, so you need to be signed in to a Google account to use it.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

You.com

Free Easy Company-made

A private search engine that answers questions with AI and shows exactly which websites the facts came from.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $15/month (billed annually; $20 monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

The free version limits how many AI searches you can do each day.

DuckDuckGo AI Chat

Free Easy Company-made

Great for asking questions in private - it does not track you, and you can switch between models like GPT, Claude, and Llama.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $9.99/month
Difficulty: Easy

Chats are anonymous, so they are not saved when you leave the page; advanced AI models need the $9.99-a-month DuckDuckGo Plus plan.

NotebookLM

Free Easy Company-made

Great for studying: upload your documents and ask it questions, or turn them into a podcast-style audio chat between two AI hosts.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

It only answers from the documents you upload, so weak sources mean weak answers.

Perplexity Comet

Free Easy Company-made

A web browser with an AI assistant built in that can summarize pages, compare products, and do small tasks for you.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $5/month
Difficulty: Easy

It collects your browsing history to target ads, and the advanced agent features need a paid plan (Comet Plus costs $5 a month).

Kimi

Free Easy Open source

A free chatbot that handles very long documents and searches the web, with open models you can also run yourself.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

It is made by a Chinese company, so think carefully about what personal details you share.
