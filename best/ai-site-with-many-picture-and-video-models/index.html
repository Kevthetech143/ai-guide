Which AI site lets me try many picture and video models in one place?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

Recraft makes and edits images with several different AI models, though its free-plan images are public with no commercial use. Scenario gives you 65+ AI models for game, product and brand art with a free tier, and Magnific puts image, video, music and voice models in one subscription but has no free plan. Replicate has thousands of models for images, video, speech, music and chat, but it is built for developers and billed per use.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Paid Medium Company-made

One subscription for AI image, video, music and voice generation with many leading models, pro editing tools, a shared canvas for workflows called Spaces, and a stock library with a commercial AI license.
