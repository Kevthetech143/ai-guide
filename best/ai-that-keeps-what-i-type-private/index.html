Which AI tools keep what I type private?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

DuckDuckGo AI Chat does not track you, and Lumo is a private chat assistant from Proton. LM Studio, Jan, and GPT4All run on your own computer, so what you type stays there as long as you use the models on your own machine (LM Studio also sells optional cloud models). Ecosia says your chats are never used to train AI models.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

LM Studio

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

It's a free desktop app that runs AI models right on your computer, so what you type stays private unless you choose its optional paid cloud models.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

You need a fairly strong computer: 16GB of memory is recommended, and 8GB works only with smaller models.

DuckDuckGo AI Chat

Free Easy Company-made

Great for asking questions in private - it does not track you, and you can switch between models like GPT, Claude, and Llama.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $9.99/month
Difficulty: Easy

The more advanced AI models need a paid DuckDuckGo subscription, which starts at $9.99 a month.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-23

GPT4All

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

A free desktop app that downloads open AI models and chats with them entirely on your computer - nothing you type ever leaves your machine.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

It is built for developers, teams, and AI power users rather than total beginners - expect to pick and configure your own models.

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

A free, open-source desktop app that runs AI chat models on your computer - like ChatGPT, but your conversations stay private on your machine.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

Bigger models are large downloads, and the online-model options connect to your own accounts with those providers.

Andi

Free Easy Company-made

Ad-free conversational answers and visual search results with a privacy-first, no-tracking positioning.

Price: Free (a separate Search API for developers is paid per query)
Difficulty: Easy

AI answers can contain mistakes (its terms say so), so check the sources it shows. Only the search site is free; the developer Search API is paid.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-28

Lumo

Free Easy Company-made

A private AI chat assistant from Proton, the email and privacy company. You can try it without an account, and a free account lets you keep your chat history.

Price: Free (Lumo Plus $12.99/month)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan limits use of the Max model, messages and chat history, and image generations, and allows 1 project. Lumo Plus raises those limits.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-07

Brave Search (Ask Brave)

Free Easy Company-made

A private search engine built on its own index. It puts AI answers that cite their sources at the top of results, and Ask Brave gives a chat-style answer with supporting websites and videos, then lets you ask follow-up questions.

Price: Free (ad-free Search Premium $3/month, or $2.50/month billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

Free searches show private ads. Search Premium, the paid ad-free option, is only available in the Brave browser.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-10

Ecosia

Free Easy Company-made

A search engine that uses all its profits for climate action and has an AI Chat tab next to web results. Ecosia says its small AI models give accurate answers from reliable sources and that your chats are never used to train AI models.

Price: Free (AI Chat Starter plan $7.99/month, Pro plan $14.99/month)
Difficulty: Easy

You can try AI Chat without signing in, but the free plan's full features need a free Ecosia account and have daily limits on file uploads and Think longer mode. Unlimited AI Chat messages need the Starter plan at $7.99/month.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-10

Qwant

Free Easy Company-made

A French search engine hosted in Europe that says it does not store your search history. For some factual questions its AI shows a short Flash Answer with source links above the results, and with a free Qwant account a Summary feature condenses a web page in one click.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

Qwant says its AI features are free and unlimited. The Flash Answer only appears for certain factual searches, not every search, and may not be switched on in every region or language yet. Qwant says the AI can be wrong, so check the sources it cites.

Official site Details
