Can AI take the vocals out of a song?

231 of 231 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: October 9, 2026.

Ultimate Vocal Remover and StemRoller split a song into vocals, drums, bass, and instruments, which is handy for karaoke tracks. Basic Pitch turns a recording of one instrument, or your singing, into a MIDI file, a note file you can edit in music software.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Ultimate Vocal Remover

Free Easy Open source Runs on your computer

A free desktop app for Windows, Mac and Linux that splits a song into vocals and instruments using AI models, for karaoke tracks and remixing.

Price: Free (donations welcome)
Difficulty: Easy

Splitting runs on your own computer, so it can be slow without a good graphics card. You download the AI models you want to use.

StemRoller

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

Type a song name and it splits the song into vocals, drums, bass and everything else, plus an instrumental. Handy for making karaoke tracks.

Price: Free (donations welcome)
Difficulty: Easy

Splitting a song takes several minutes. You download the app and it runs on your own computer.

Basic Pitch

Free Easy Open source Runs on your computer

Drop in a recording of one instrument, or sing into your microphone, and get a MIDI file back that you can edit in music software. It is a free tool from Spotify.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

The online tool takes a recording of a single instrument or your singing, and gives you a MIDI file to clean up, not finished sheet music. There is also a version for programmers that you install with pip.
