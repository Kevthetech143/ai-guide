Can I make AI pictures on my own computer?

Last full review: 2026-10-10

Stable Diffusion and Flux make pictures from a written description and can run on your own computer. DiffusionBee is a simple Stable Diffusion app for Mac, and Draw Things makes pictures on an iPhone, iPad, or Mac without the internet. Fooocus offers simple text-to-picture on your own machine.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Stable Diffusion

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

Free AI picture-making that runs on your own computer with no subscription and no picture limits.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

You have to install it yourself and your computer needs a good graphics card.

Flux

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

It makes very detailed pictures from a short written description, like posters, art, or product mockups.

Price: Free; pay-per-use options
Difficulty: Medium

Setting it up on your own computer takes some technical steps and a strong graphics card.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

ComfyUI

Free Hard Open source Runs on your computer

The open standard for serious AI image and video work - connect models and processing steps as visible nodes on a canvas for total control over every step.

Price: Free to run yourself (cloud from $16/month billed annually)
Difficulty: Hard

Free on your own computer, but their hosted Comfy Cloud starts at $16 a month on yearly billing. The node-canvas workflow takes real learning - start from a community template.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-21

DiffusionBee

Free Easy Open source Runs on your computer

The easiest way to run Stable Diffusion on a Mac - text-to-image, generative fill, upscaling, and video tools in one free app that works fully offline.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

Mac only, and Apple silicon is recommended - Intel Macs work but generate images much more slowly.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-21

Upscayl

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

AI image upscaling on desktop (Linux, macOS, Windows); cloud plans add AI image generation.

Price: Free to run yourself (Pro cloud from $9.99/month)
Difficulty: Easy

The free desktop app needs a graphics card that supports Vulkan; many built-in (integrated) laptop graphics chips do not work.

Official site Details

Fooocus

Free Easy Open source Runs on your computer

Simple, offline text-to-image on your own computer: type a description, fix parts of a picture, or add a style.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

Needs 4GB+ Nvidia VRAM, 8GB RAM, 40GB free disk; project is in limited long-term support (bug fixes only).

AI photo enhancement: denoise, sharpen, upscale, face recovery.

Price: $39/month (Personal, billed monthly), or $199/year

Commercial use limited to orgs under $1M USD annual revenue.

Draw Things

Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

A free image-making app for iPhone, iPad and Mac that creates pictures from text on your own device, offline. It can also train your own custom styles on the device.

Price: Free (Draw Things+ $8.99/month for more cloud power)
Difficulty: Medium

It is built for Apple devices. Cloud compute needs a free account, and higher cloud limits and custom style uploads need Draw Things+ at $8.99 a month.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-06

SwarmUI

Free Hard Company-made Runs on your computer

A web page you run on your own computer for making AI images and video with models like Stable Diffusion and Flux. Gives you a lot of control.

Price: Free to run yourself
Difficulty: Hard

It is aimed at people comfortable installing software and picking models. Each model you download has its own license.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-06

Easy Diffusion

Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

A one-click installer that sets up Stable Diffusion on your own computer, with a friendly page for making pictures from text or from another image.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

It needs at least 8 GB of memory and 25 GB of free disk space. It can use an NVIDIA, AMD or Apple graphics chip, or run more slowly without one. The license forbids sharing images that break laws or cause harm.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-06

Krita AI Diffusion

Free Hard Company-made Runs on your computer

A free plugin for the Krita painting app that adds AI image generation, inpainting and outpainting inside your drawing. Runs on your own hardware.

Price: Free to run yourself (online service: token packs from €10, one-time)
Difficulty: Hard

You need to install Krita first and then set up the plugin. Running it on your own computer needs a strong graphics card with at least 6 GB of graphics memory, or it gets very slow. The online service needs no graphics card, but you pay for tokens after the free ones run out.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-06

Stability Matrix

Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

A free app that installs and manages popular AI image tools such as ComfyUI, Fooocus and Forge with one click, and lets you browse and download models from CivitAI and Hugging Face.

Price: Free (optional supporter plans from $5/month)
Difficulty: Medium

It sets up other image tools rather than being one itself, and you download the image models separately. Paid supporter plans add cloud extras and early builds; the $5/month Supporter plan includes 1,000 credits.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-08

SD.Next

Free Hard Company-made Runs on your computer

A free, open-source web interface for AI image and video generation on your own computer. It supports many models, which you pick from a list and it downloads automatically.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Hard

Windows users can use the all-in-one installer and launcher; on other systems you install it from the terminal with git and a launch script. The README lists support for Nvidia, AMD, Intel Arc, Apple M1/M2 and CPU-only setups, so check its install guide for your hardware.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-08

WanGP

Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

One free local app for AI video, image and music with models such as Wan 2.2, LTX-2, Qwen Image, Flux and ACE-Step, using a browser interface.

Price: Free to run on your own computer
Difficulty: Medium

Its site says some models run on as little as 6 GB of video memory; others need more. The license lets you use it free but not sell WanGP itself or offer it as a paid hosted service.
