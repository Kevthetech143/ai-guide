Can AI turn a photo into a video?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

Luma Dream Machine can make a still photo move, and Runway, Pika, Kling, and Hailuo AI turn a photo or a written prompt into a short clip. Several of them limit clips to about 10 seconds, and some free plans add a watermark.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Runway

Free Easy Company-made

It turns your words or photos into short video clips for ads, social posts, or creative projects.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $12/month (billed annually; $15 monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

You spend credits for each second of video, so longer videos use them up fast.

Veo

Free Medium Company-made

Google's top video maker; it turns words or photos into sharp, cinematic video clips, even with matching sound.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $4.99/month (Google AI Plus)
Difficulty: Medium

The free plan gives you 50 Google Flow credits a day, and each video uses credits; the cheapest paid plan (Google AI Plus) costs $4.99 a month, and prices may vary by country.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-21

Pika

Paid Easy Company-made

It turns your words or photos into short, fun video clips with playful effects like melting or exploding objects.

Price: $8/month (billed annually; $10 monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan gives you no monthly credits, so you buy credit packs to make clips; the cheapest plan (Starter) costs $10 a month, or $8 if you pay for a year.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-21

Kling

Free Easy Company-made

It turns words or photos into smooth, realistic-looking video clips, and the free Basic plan lets you try it.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $10/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free Basic plan has a watermark and no commercial use; paid plans start at $10 a month.

Luma Dream Machine

Paid Easy Company-made

It turns a written description or a photo into a realistic short video clip, like making a still photo move or describing a scene and watching it come to life.

Price: $30/month
Difficulty: Easy

Clips are short (about 5 to 10 seconds each), and the paid Plus plan costs $30 a month, so you likely need to pay before you can create anything.

Hailuo AI

Free Easy Company-made

It turns your words or photos into short AI videos in just a few clicks.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $14.99/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan only gives you one-time trial credits, so you need a paid plan to keep making videos; the cheapest paid plan (Standard) costs $14.99 a month.

Vidu

Free Easy Company-made

Make short AI videos from text, from a photo, or from reference pictures of a character or object.

Price: Free (Standard $10/month)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan is limited, for example 10 saved references a month. Standard includes 800 credits a month, which the page says covers up to 200 videos.

DomoAI

Paid Easy Company-made

Turn text or pictures into short AI videos and images. The Basic plan includes character-to-video, talking avatars, 4K video upscaling and watermark-free results.

Price: $13/month (Basic)
Difficulty: Easy

Basic includes 600 credits a month, which the page says is about 85 videos or 150 images. Unlimited "Relax" generations on supported models start with the Standard plan at $42/month.
