Can AI remove the background from a photo?

231 of 231 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: October 9, 2026.

Remove.bg removes the background from a photo in seconds with one click. Photoroom, Pixelcut, and Clipdrop can do it too, and Clipdrop's free plan allows 20 uses per 24 hours.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Remove.bg

Free Easy Company-made

Removes the background from any photo in seconds with one click - great for product shots and profile pictures.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $9/month
Difficulty: Easy

The remove.bg website closes on 1 December 2026 and its background remover moves into Canva; until then, top quality needs credits ($3 for 3) or the $9-a-month plan.

Pixelcut

Free Easy Company-made

Remove backgrounds, erase objects, upscale photos and generate new backgrounds with AI, in a simple editor.

Price: Free (Pro $10/month, or $8/month billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

On the free plan background removal and upscaling are limited. Pro includes 600 AI credits a month, and the stronger AI models spend those credits.

Clipdrop

Free Easy Company-made

Free web tools to remove backgrounds, erase unwanted objects, upscale pictures 2x, relight photos and remove text.

Price: Free (Pro $15/month)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan allows 20 uses per 24 hours on background removal, upscaling, cleanup and relight, and 50 on text remover. Text-to-image, extending photos (Uncrop) and background replacement need Pro at $15/month, which also adds high-resolution output and 16x upscaling.
