Which AI makes the best logos?

Last full review: 2026-09-26

Ideogram is unusually good at putting readable words inside a design, which is exactly what a logo needs. Adobe Firefly and Canva AI also have free plans that work well for quick logo ideas and mockups.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Ideogram

Free Easy Open source

Great for making logos, posters, and designs that include words — it spells text inside pictures better than most other tools.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $15/month
Difficulty: Easy

Pictures you make on the free plan are public for everyone to see, and you can't keep them private without paying; paid plans start at $15 a month.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

Adobe Firefly

Free Easy Company-made

Great for business-ready pictures that are safe to use commercially, since it is trained on licensed content.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $9.99/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan only gives 25 credits a month, and unused credits do not roll over to the next month.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

Canva AI (Magic Studio)

Free Easy Company-made

Great for making social media posts, flyers, and presentations with AI help, right inside an easy drag-and-drop design tool.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $15/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan only gives a small number of AI uses each month, and Canva prices change by country, so check the pricing page for your own price.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

Remove.bg

Free Easy Company-made

Removes the background from any photo in seconds with one click - great for product shots and profile pictures.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $9/month
Difficulty: Easy

Free downloads are limited and smaller; full-quality images need credits or a plan.

Playground AI

Free Easy Company-made

Type a description and it creates images for you, with lots of ready-made style templates to start from.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $15/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan is slow and limited to a few images every few hours, and the premium models use monthly credits.

Free Easy Company-made

Editing photos and generating design images with simple text prompts.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $8.99/month
Difficulty: Easy

The $8.99/month plan is credit-based, and extra top-up credits cost more on top.

DiffusionBee

Free Easy Open source Runs on your computer

The easiest way to run Stable Diffusion on a Mac - text-to-image, generative fill, upscaling, and video tools in one free app that works fully offline.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

Mac only, and Apple silicon is recommended - Intel Macs work but generate images much more slowly.
