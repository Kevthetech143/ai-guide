Is there an AI that finds scientific papers?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

Semantic Scholar is a free AI search across scientific papers, with a one-line summary of each. Consensus answers from peer-reviewed papers, and Elicit builds evidence tables from over 138 million academic papers. Semantic Scholar covers academic papers only, so it is not a general web search.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Elicit

Free Easy Company-made

Ask a research question and get structured evidence tables pulled from over 138 million academic papers.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $11/month (billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan has limited usage for the Research Agent and Research Reports, and the $11/month price is billed as $132 a year.

Paid Easy Company-made

Check whether scientific papers are supported or contradicted by later research, with AI-written summaries.

Price: $12/month (billed yearly)
Difficulty: Easy

There is no ongoing free plan, only a 7-day free trial that turns into a paid subscription unless you cancel.

Free Easy Company-made

Free AI search across scientific papers, with one-line summaries of each paper and links to the papers it cites.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

Academic literature only — it is not a general web search engine.

Consensus

Free Easy Company-made

Evidence-backed answers and AI summaries drawn directly from peer-reviewed papers, with unlimited Pro messages.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from Pro from $20/month
Difficulty: Easy

$20/month is the monthly price ($12/month if you pay for a year). Pro includes 15 Deep reviews a month; the Free plan gets 10 Pro messages and up to 3 Deep reviews a month.

Undermind

Free Easy Company-made

A search tool for scientific papers: you describe your research, it reads hundreds of papers and builds a report with citations you can trace back to the source.

Price: Free (Pro $20/month for academic users, $75/month for industry users)
Difficulty: Easy

Pro costs $20 a month for academic users but $75 a month for industry users; paying yearly lowers the academic price to $16 a month. The free plan has standard usage limits; Pro raises them 10 times.

ResearchRabbit

Free Easy Company-made

Find research papers related to ones you already like. The free plan has unlimited searches across 310+ million articles and unlimited collections you can share.

Price: Free (ResearchRabbit+ $12.50/month)
Difficulty: Easy

Free allows up to 50 seed articles. Up to 300 seed articles, advanced search controls and multiple projects need ResearchRabbit+ at $12.50/month ($10/month on the annual plan).

Anara

Free Easy Company-made

Search the scientific literature, then ask questions across your own uploaded papers and files and get answers that point to the exact passage they came from.

Price: Free (Plus plan $10/month, or $8/month billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan has limited AI usage and uploads (20MB or 120 pages per upload). Deep Search for multi-step research needs the Max 5x plan at $100/month on monthly billing.
