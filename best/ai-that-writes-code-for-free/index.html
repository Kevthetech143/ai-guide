Which AI writes code for free?

Last full review: 2026-09-14

GitHub Copilot and Replit have free plans that write code from your descriptions and fix errors for you. Cursor's free tier is generous too, and it is one of the easiest ways to start.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Cursor

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

It is an app where an AI assistant helps you write, fix, and explain code right in your editor.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $20/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan only includes a small number of AI requests each month.

Devin

Free Medium Company-made

An AI software engineer that can plan, write, and test code for whole projects on its own in the cloud.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $20/month
Difficulty: Medium

It charges for the computer time it uses, so a big job can cost more than the $20 starting price.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

GitHub Copilot

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

It suggests code as you type right inside your editor, and it can explain code and help fix bugs.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $10/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan only includes a limited number of completions and chats each month.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

Replit

Free Easy Company-made

You describe an app in plain words and its AI agent builds it and puts it on the web, all in your browser.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $18/month (billed annually; $25 monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan only includes small daily AI credits, and extra AI usage costs more.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

Windsurf

Free Medium Company-made

An AI code editor that understands your whole project and can change many files at once, with you watching each step.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $20/month
Difficulty: Medium

The free plan gives you a limited number of AI prompts each month; the Pro plan costs $20 a month. Windsurf is now part of Devin (Devin Desktop).

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

Lovable

Free Easy Company-made

Great for non-programmers: describe the app or website you want in plain words and it builds a working version for you.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $25/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan only gives you a few builds each day, and big projects use up credits fast; the Pro plan costs $25 a month.
