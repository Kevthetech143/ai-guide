Can AI copy a voice?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

Fish Audio can copy a voice from as little as 10 seconds of audio. Hume AI also mentions voice cloning, while Orpheus TTS and F5-TTS run on your own computer and need Python, a programming tool. Only copy voices you have permission to use.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

ACE Studio

Paid Medium Company-made

AI singing voices from your melody and lyrics, voice cloning, and AI instruments that work alongside music-making software.

Price: $20.73/month Artist (billed annually)
Difficulty: Medium

Generative kits, stem splitter, and video tools are metered from a 2,500 credits/month pool on the Artist plan.

Fish Audio

Free Easy Open source Runs on your computer

Natural-sounding text-to-speech and voice cloning from as little as 10 seconds of audio, in 30+ languages; the company also shares downloadable models you can run yourself.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $15/month (Plus)
Difficulty: Easy

Free Tier is for personal use only; monetizing content requires a paid plan, and unused credits do not roll over.

Hume AI

Free Easy Company-made

AI voices that react to your tone: spoken back-and-forth chat, text-to-speech and voice cloning, mainly for people building apps.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $3/month (Starter)
Difficulty: Easy

Starter includes about 30 minutes of text-to-speech and 40 minutes of AI voice chat a month; the price table shows no pay-for-more rate on Starter, so heavier use means upgrading.

Last checked 2026-09-28

Orpheus TTS

Free Hard Open source Runs on your computer

Free, expressive text-to-speech you can run yourself, with emotion tags (like laughing or sighing) and voice cloning from a short sample.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Hard

There is no app to click: you install it with Python commands and run it as code. The main models are English; other languages are only a research preview.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-28

F5-TTS

Free Hard Open source Runs on your computer

An open text-to-speech model that can copy the sound of a voice from a short sample recording. Runs on your own computer.

Price: Free to run yourself
Difficulty: Hard

The code is MIT licensed but the voice model files are licensed for non-commercial use only. Only copy voices you have permission to use. Setup needs Python.
