What's the best AI for making videos?

Last full review: 2026-09-14

Runway, Sora, Pika, and Kling turn text into short videos, and HeyGen can put a realistic presenter on screen for you. Most are free to try, with paid plans for longer or sharper videos.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Runway

Free Easy Company-made

It turns your words or photos into short video clips for ads, social posts, or creative projects.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $12/month (billed annually; $15 monthly)
Difficulty: Easy

You spend credits for each second of video, so longer videos use them up fast.

Sora

Free Easy Company-made

It turns your written descriptions into short, realistic-looking video clips, even with matching sound.

Price: $20/month (ChatGPT Plus; free with daily limits)
Difficulty: Easy

Free users only get a limited number of clips each day, and longer videos without watermarks need a paid plan.

Veo

Paid Medium Company-made

Google's top video maker; it turns words or photos into sharp, cinematic video clips, even with matching sound.

Price: $20/month (Google AI Pro)
Difficulty: Medium

The cheapest official way in is the Google AI Pro plan at $20 a month; you pay per second of video, so costs add up fast.

Pika

Free Easy Company-made

It turns your words or photos into short, fun video clips with playful effects like melting or exploding objects.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $8/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan gives you only a few short clips a month, and free clips carry a watermark.

Kling

Free Easy Company-made

It turns words or photos into smooth, realistic-looking video clips, and you get free credits every day.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $10/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free daily credits only cover short clips, and longer or watermark-free videos need a paid plan.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

HeyGen

Free Easy Company-made

Turns your written text into a video with a realistic AI talking person, so you can make studio-quality videos without a camera, actors, or editing skills.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $29/month
Difficulty: Easy

You pay with credits and each video costs credits, so making lots of videos can get expensive; the free plan also only gives 3 videos a month and adds a watermark.

Luma Dream Machine

Paid Easy Company-made

It turns a written description or a photo into a realistic short video clip, like making a still photo move or describing a scene and watching it come to life.

Price: $30/month
Difficulty: Easy

Clips are short (about 5 to 10 seconds each), and the paid Plus plan costs $30 a month, so you likely need to pay before you can create anything.

CapCut AI

Free Easy Company-made

A free video editor from the maker of TikTok with AI captions, background removal, and text-to-speech built right in.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $9.99/month
Difficulty: Easy

Some features and cloud storage need the paid Pro plan; the paid Standard plan costs about $9.99 a month, and it is best for short videos, not full movies.
