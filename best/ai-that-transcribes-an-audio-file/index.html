Can AI turn an interview or audio file into text?

231 of 231 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: October 9, 2026.

Sonix transcribes audio in 54+ languages and lets you edit the text in your browser. Buzz and Vibe are free apps that turn audio and video files into text on your own computer. Whisper does this too, but you have to install it yourself with command-line steps.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Whisper

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

It turns spoken audio into written text, great for transcribing meetings, lectures, or video subtitles. It understands dozens of languages.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

You have to install it yourself with some command-line steps, and it needs a decent computer to run well.

Sonix

Paid Easy Company-made

Automated transcription in 54+ languages with in-browser editing, translation, and subtitle exports.

Price: $25/month (Core)
Difficulty: Easy

Core includes 1 user and 5 hrs/month of transcription; extra seats are +$25/month each.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-27

Buzz

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

A free app that turns audio and video into text, and translates it, entirely on your own computer using OpenAI's Whisper. Nothing has to be uploaded.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

Buzz now needs an Apple silicon Mac; version 1.4.5 is the last one that runs on Intel Macs. The Windows installer is not signed, so you will see a warning when you install it.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-10-29

Vibe

Free Easy Company-made Runs on your computer

A free app that transcribes audio and video files into text on your own device, with subtitles export and speaker labels. Works on Mac, Windows and Linux.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

Transcription speed depends on your computer. Optional summaries use the Claude API or Ollama, which you set up separately.
