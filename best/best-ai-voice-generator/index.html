What's the best AI voice generator?

ElevenLabs has the most natural-sounding AI voices around, and Murf AI is great for narration. Both have free tiers so you can hear them before spending anything.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

ElevenLabs

Free Easy Company-made

It turns your written words into natural-sounding spoken audio, great for videos, podcasts, or audiobooks.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $5/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan is small, so you will hit the monthly limit quickly if you make lots of audio.

Whisper

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

It turns spoken audio into written text, great for transcribing meetings, lectures, or video subtitles. It understands dozens of languages.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

You have to install it yourself with some command-line steps, and it needs a decent computer to run well.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

Murf AI

Free Easy Company-made

Murf is best at turning typed scripts into natural-sounding voiceovers for videos, podcasts, and presentations.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $19/month
Difficulty: Easy

On the free plan you cannot download your audio or use it in anything commercial — downloads and commercial rights need a paid plan.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

PlayAI (formerly PlayHT)

Paid Easy Company-made

It turns your written text into realistic spoken audio using over 600 voices in 142 languages and accents.

Price: No monthly price listed — check the provider's site
Difficulty: Easy

It is priced by how much audio you make, so heavy use or voice cloning can get expensive fast.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-11

Otter.ai

Free Easy Company-made

Joins your meetings and writes down what everyone says, so you get notes and summaries without typing a word.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $16.99/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan only covers a limited number of meeting minutes, and it needs access to join your calls; the Pro plan costs $16.99 a month.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

Descript

Free Easy Company-made

Great for editing podcasts and videos by editing the written transcript - delete a sentence and it is gone from the recording, like editing a document.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $16/month (billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan only includes about one hour of editing a month; paid plans start at $16 a month when billed yearly.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

NotebookLM

Free Easy Company-made

Great for studying: upload your documents and ask it questions, or turn them into a podcast-style audio chat between two AI hosts.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Easy

It only answers from the documents you upload, so weak sources mean weak answers.
