Can AI make background music for my videos?

197 of 232 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: September 19, 2026.

Mubert and Soundful make royalty-free background music for videos. Ecrett Music lets you pick a scene, mood, and style. Check the free plans first: Mubert's adds an audible watermark and asks for credit, and Ecrett Music's free plan only downloads previews you may not publish.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Mubert

Free Easy Company-made

Generate royalty-free background music from a text prompt for videos, podcasts, and apps.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $11.69/month (billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan adds an audible watermark and requires attribution, and tracks can't be released on Spotify or registered with Content ID.

Soundraw

Free Easy Company-made

It turns a genre, mood, and length you pick into an original royalty-free track - the AI is trained only on music their own producers write and record, and every download comes with a worldwide, perpetual license.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $16.99/month ($11.04/month billed annually)
Difficulty: Easy

The cheapest Creator plan only downloads MP3s. WAV files and separate instrument stems need the Artist Pro plan - Artist Starter is MP3-only too.

Free Easy Company-made

Making simple background music for videos, games and podcasts: pick a scene, mood and style, and its AI makes a new tune you can use without paying extra each time

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $7.99/month (Individual), or $4.99/month billed annually
