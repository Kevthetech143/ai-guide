Which AI tools does OpenAI make?

Last full review: 2026-10-10

ChatGPT is good for chatting, writing, homework help and planning trips, and ChatGPT agent goes further and browses the web, fills out forms and compares prices, but it needs a paid ChatGPT plan. Sora turns written descriptions into short video clips, and Whisper turns spoken audio into text, though you install it yourself. OpenAI Codex is for programmers, not beginners, and also needs a paid plan.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

ChatGPT

Free Easy Company-made

Great for chatting, writing, homework help, planning trips, and turning word descriptions into pictures.

Price: Free tier available; paid plans from $8/month
Difficulty: Easy

The free plan is limited and can show ads; the cheapest paid plan (Go) costs $8 a month.

Whisper

Free Medium Open source Runs on your computer

It turns spoken audio into written text, great for transcribing meetings, lectures, or video subtitles. It understands dozens of languages.

Price: Free
Difficulty: Medium

You have to install it yourself with some command-line steps, and it needs a decent computer to run well.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

Sora

Free Easy Company-made

It turns your written descriptions into short, realistic-looking video clips, even with matching sound.

Price: $20/month (ChatGPT Plus; free with daily limits)
Difficulty: Easy

Free users only get a limited number of clips each day, and longer videos without watermarks need a paid plan.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

OpenAI Codex

Paid Medium Company-made

Great for programmers: it works inside your terminal, writes new features, fixes bugs, and runs your tests for you.

Price: $20/month (with ChatGPT Plus)
Difficulty: Medium

It is a tool for people who code, not beginners; you need a paid ChatGPT plan to use it.

Official site Details

Last checked 2026-09-14

ChatGPT agent

Paid Easy Company-made

Great for multi-step online chores: it browses the web, fills out forms, compares prices, and finishes tasks for you.

Price: $20/month (with ChatGPT Plus)
Difficulty: Easy

It needs a paid ChatGPT plan, and you should check its work before it books or buys anything.
