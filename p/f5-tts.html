F5-TTS

Made by SWivid with the X-LANCE, SII and PCL labs

Free Hard Open source Runs on your computer

An open text-to-speech model that can copy the sound of a voice from a short sample recording. Runs on your own computer.

  • PriceFree to run yourself
  • DifficultyHard
  • Good forVoice

The code is MIT licensed but the voice model files are licensed for non-commercial use only. Only copy voices you have permission to use. Setup needs Python.
