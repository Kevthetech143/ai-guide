Provider

YuE2

Made by Multimodal Art Projection (M-A-P)

Free Hard Open source Runs on your computer

An open music model that turns your lyrics and a style description into a full song with vocals and backing music. The model files are free to download and run on your own computer.

  • PriceFree to run yourself
  • DifficultyHard
  • Good forMusic

Running it yourself needs a Linux computer with an NVIDIA graphics card with 24 GB of memory. Companies need a separate commercial license for the model files.
