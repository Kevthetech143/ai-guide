Krita AI Diffusion

Made by Acly and contributors (online service: Cosmic Interstice UG)

Free Hard Company-made Runs on your computer

A free plugin for the Krita painting app that adds AI image generation, inpainting and outpainting inside your drawing. Runs on your own hardware.

  • PriceFree to run yourself (online service: token packs from €10, one-time)
  • DifficultyHard
  • Good forImage

You need to install Krita first and then set up the plugin. Running it on your own computer needs a strong graphics card with at least 6 GB of graphics memory, or it gets very slow. The online service needs no graphics card, but you pay for tokens after the free ones run out.
