Open WebUI

Made by Open WebUI, Inc.

Free Hard Company-made Runs on your computer

A free chat app for AI that you install yourself and use in your web browser. It works with AI models on your own computer (through Ollama) or online ones, and adds document chat, web search, voice and image tools.

  • PriceFree to run yourself (Enterprise plans are by quote)
  • DifficultyHard
  • Good forChat

You install it yourself with a typed command (pip) or Docker, then connect an AI model. Business licenses, for rebranding and dedicated support, are sold only to companies.
