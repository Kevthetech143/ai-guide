Easy Diffusion

Made by cmdr2 and contributors

Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

A one-click installer that sets up Stable Diffusion on your own computer, with a friendly page for making pictures from text or from another image.

  • PriceFree
  • DifficultyMedium
  • Good forImage

It needs at least 8 GB of memory and 25 GB of free disk space. It can use an NVIDIA, AMD or Apple graphics chip, or run more slowly without one. The license forbids sharing images that break laws or cause harm.
