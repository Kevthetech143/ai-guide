Provider

Dia2

Made by Nari Labs

Free Hard Open source Runs on your computer

An open model that turns a script with two speakers into natural-sounding spoken dialogue, and can start speaking before the whole text is in.

  • PriceFree to run yourself
  • DifficultyHard
  • Good forVoice

It only supports English and up to 2 minutes of audio at a time. Voices change from one try to the next unless you give it a voice sample. Setup needs a command line, and the guide is written for computers with an NVIDIA graphics card.
