Free Medium Company-made Runs on your computer

Keeps your screen, audio and meetings on your own computer so you can search what you saw or heard, and share chosen context with AI tools such as Claude.

  • PriceFree (Basic $25/month for full history)
  • DifficultyMedium
  • Good forSearch

It records your screen and audio, so think about what is on screen. The free plan searches recent work only; searching your full history needs Basic at $25/month.
