Piper

Made by Open Home Foundation

Free Hard Open source Runs on your computer

A fast text-to-speech engine that runs fully on your own computer, with many free downloadable voices. Good for reading text aloud without sending it to the cloud.

  • PriceFree to run yourself
  • DifficultyHard
  • Good forVoice

You install it from the command line, so it suits technical users. The project says it is looking for new maintainers.
