(a) photoroom free_tier — KEEP true. The pricing page has no Free card, only "Start free trial", but Photoroom's own help center says it plainly: "You can use a limited version of Photoroom for free by creating an account. This gives you a Space on the Free plan with limited features, AI credits, and export capacity." (help.photoroom.com/en/articles/13366906-free-trials-of-photoroom, updated ~3 weeks ago) and "Free accounts are limited to personal use only" (help.photoroom.com/en/articles/12523455-free-accounts-and-commercial-use). The 7-day trial is a separate thing "on yearly Pro and Max plans only". So a real free plan exists; no card change. The watch_out stays as is (every sentence on the cited pricing page). Next batch: if a reader-facing free-plan note is wanted, the help article above is the source.
(b) jetbrains-ai — ADD the FAQ limit; APPLIED. The card headline says "Free (3 AI Credits per 30 days)" but the free AI plan does not work in the free IntelliJ IDEA or PyCharm, or in Android Studio — exactly the editors a non-technical reader is most likely to have. The sentence is on the already-cited page (store/ai FAQ, plus the plan card's "Only available for commercial IDE versions"), so the 018v rule is met and watch_out_source stays https://www.jetbrains.com/store/ai/. Plain-words rewrite in section 3.
(c) cline watch_out_source — KEEP the worker's https://cline.bot/cline-pass. Neither page carries the whole sentence: /pricing has "free", "inference credits" and BYOK OpenAI/Anthropic but says "no subscriptions" (a reader sent there would not find the $9.99 and would read the card as wrong); /cline-pass has the $9.99/month, the pay-as-you-go Cline provider line and "no separate provider setup or API keys". The ClinePass price is the fact a reader most needs to see for themselves, and /pricing flatly contradicts it, so /cline-pass is the better of two imperfect URLs. If the lead wants one page to back every clause, the only clean option is to drop the OpenAI/Anthropic clause from watch_out; I did not, because it is true and useful.
