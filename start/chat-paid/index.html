229 of 229 listings checked in the last 30 days. Oldest check: November 16, 2026.

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device. Some, like coding helpers, still send what you type to an online AI. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

HuggingChat

Paid Easy Open source

Chatting with many open-source AI models in one app, using a Hugging Face account.

Price: $9/month (Hugging Face PRO)
Difficulty: Easy

You need a Hugging Face account, and Hugging Face says free accounts get no monthly credits for the models behind it, so count on the $9-a-month PRO plan or buying credits.
