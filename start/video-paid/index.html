Paid Video

Badges, in plain words: Open source — anyone can download and inspect how it works. Company-made — a company runs it for you. Free — you can use the main features without paying. Paid — you need to pay, usually a monthly plan. Runs on your computer — it runs on your own device, nothing sent to the cloud. Easy / Medium / Hard — how tricky it is to set up and use.

Veo

Paid Medium Company-made

Google's top video maker; it turns words or photos into sharp, cinematic video clips, even with matching sound.

Price: $20/month (Google AI Pro)
Difficulty: Medium

The cheapest official way in is the Google AI Pro plan at $20 a month; you pay per second of video, so costs add up fast.

Luma Dream Machine

Paid Easy Company-made

It turns a written description or a photo into a realistic short video clip, like making a still photo move or describing a scene and watching it come to life.

Price: $30/month
Difficulty: Easy

Clips are short (about 5 to 10 seconds each), and the paid Plus plan costs $30 a month, so you likely need to pay before you can create anything.
