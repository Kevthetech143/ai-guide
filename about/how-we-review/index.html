How we review

We try to keep every provider on this site up to date.

Who looks after this site

This site is maintained by one person, not a company. We aim to re-check every listing at least once every 30 days. We go through them a few at a time, not all on one day. If a listing goes past 30 days, its card says "Needs a new check" until we look again. Each page also shows how many listings were checked in the last 30 days.

Nobody pays to be listed

No company pays to be listed here. There are no ads on this site. There are no affiliate links. There is no paid placement. We do not take money from any provider on this site.

What "last checked" means

Every card on this site shows a "last checked" date. That date is the last time we looked at the provider's facts again. A price can change between checks. Always double-check the price on the provider's own page before you pay.

Where prices come from

Our prices come from the provider's own pricing page. When we cannot confirm a price from an official source, we keep the old price and say nothing new.

A time we held back price changes

On September 14, 2026, we found new prices for three providers: Kimi, PlayHT, and Gemini. We did not publish them. The Kimi price came from a single LinkedIn post. The PlayHT sources disagreed with each other, and PlayHT's official page would not load. The Gemini price came from a Singapore page, and we could not confirm the US price. We kept the old prices and will check again.
