How this guide works

A human being maintains this guide — not an AI writing about itself.

How providers get checked

Every provider is looked up on its own official site: what it costs, how hard it is to use, and what it is good for, written in plain words. Every page is stamped with the date it was last checked. Prices and plans change fast — if something looks off, treat the provider’s own site as the truth.

No ads, no affiliate links, no tracking

Nobody pays to be listed here. The “official site” buttons go straight to the provider — this guide earns nothing when you click. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no analytics following you around.

Plain language, always

If a technical word has to appear, it gets an explanation next to it. “Open source” means anyone can download and inspect how it works; “Company-made” means a company runs it for you.
